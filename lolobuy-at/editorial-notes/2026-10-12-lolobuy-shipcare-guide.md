## Editorial limitations

- ShipCare availability, coverage combinations, rates and compensation limits are route-specific. The article publishes the current official rate range only as a dated fact and directs readers to the live parcel screen for the actual offer.
- The official ShipCare page contains both a three-day post-receipt limit for damage/partial loss and broader wording about valid claim periods. The article uses the explicit three-day ShipCare deadline and recommends immediate filing.
- Loss, damage and customs-seizure coverages generally cannot be claimed repeatedly for the same event; delay may be additional where the selected line’s terms allow it.
- Electronics and 3C products are described as excluded from damage/partial-loss compensation. The article does not imply that other coverage types apply automatically.
- No claim outcome, compensation amount, delivery time, customs result, safety conclusion, private order or test purchase was used.
- Published copy contains no LoloBuy, carrier, marketplace, social, review or source links. Its only rendered external link is to `https://findspreadsheet.com/`.
