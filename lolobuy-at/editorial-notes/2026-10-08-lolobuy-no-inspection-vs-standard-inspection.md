## Editorial limitations

- The standard-inspection page opens by saying there are three inspection types, but the accessible page text clearly labels only “No Quality Inspection Needed” and “Warehouse Stock-in with Standard Quality Inspection.” The article compares only those two clearly documented stock-in choices and treats secondary inspection as a later recovery service.
- No fixed fee was published for secondary inspection or 1688 sampling. Readers are instructed to use the live order or warehouse screen for current availability, scope and charge.
- “Free” is limited to the current official description of standard inspection and is not presented as a zero-cost order or parcel.
- No authenticity, function, fit, complete wholesale-count, seller-remedy or damage-prevention guarantee is implied.
- No customer review, private order, price, delivery time, destination rule or test purchase was used.
- Published copy intentionally contains no LoloBuy, seller, marketplace, carrier, social, review or source links. Its only rendered external link is to `https://findspreadsheet.com/`.
