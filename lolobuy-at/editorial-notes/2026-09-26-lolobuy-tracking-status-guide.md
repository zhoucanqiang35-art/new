## Editorial limitations

- No current carrier scan, private order, customer anecdote, delivery promise, compensation result or fixed “lost parcel” threshold was used.
- The public help pages contain multiple timing statements for different receipt and after-sales situations. The article therefore directs readers to the live validity period instead of publishing one universal deadline.
- “Wait” guidance is conditional and does not override an explicit payment, customs, address, return or delivery action shown in the live parcel record.
- Published copy intentionally contains no LoloBuy, carrier, government, social or source links. Its only rendered external link is to `https://findspreadsheet.com/`.
