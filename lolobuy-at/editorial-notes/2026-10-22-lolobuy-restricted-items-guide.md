## Editorial limits

- The public article does not reproduce an exhaustive legal list or claim that an unlisted product is permitted.
- Historical law dates, old route names, prices, percentages, quantity ratios and liquid-volume examples in legacy help text were excluded because they may not represent current route rules.
- No method for concealing, mixing, renaming or misdeclaring a prohibited or restricted item is provided.
- Route display is not treated as a guarantee of carrier acceptance or destination-country import clearance.
- Destination law is outside the article’s scope; regulated products require current authority or professional guidance.
- Official source URLs stay in this repository-only note. The public article’s only rendered external link is `https://findspreadsheet.com/`.
