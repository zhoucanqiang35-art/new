## Editorial limitations

- No fixed handling rate, currency rate, international freight quote, tax threshold, delivery time, promotion, customer outcome or payment-method availability promise was published.
- Current public help materials contain conflicting storage-period statements. Storage duration and storage fees were excluded from this article.
- The public payment-method list is presented as help-centre context; the article tells readers that their live transaction screen controls actual availability.
- “Zero service fee” is limited to the standard purchasing-service statement and is not presented as a zero-cost order.
- Published copy intentionally contains no LoloBuy, seller, bank, carrier, competitor, social, review or source links. Its only rendered external link is to `https://findspreadsheet.com/`.
