## Editorial limits

- No claim that every seller accepts customization, supplies a proof, follows a universal production method, or permits changes after production starts.
- No fixed price, production time, domestic-delivery time, international-delivery time or outcome is published.
- The article does not claim that customized products are always returnable or never returnable; current seller terms and case-specific approval control.
- Warehouse photos are presented only as evidence of visible details within LoloBuy's published inspection scope, not as proof of colour accuracy, durability, function, authenticity or manufacturing quality.
