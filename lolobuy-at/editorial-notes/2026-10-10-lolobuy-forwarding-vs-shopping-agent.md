## Editorial limitations

- Current official pages contain conflicting warehouse-location references (Guangdong, Quanzhou and Chengdu). The published guide deliberately gives no warehouse location and directs readers to the address displayed in the live, approved Forwarding order.
- “Inspection check” is described differently across forwarding help pages. The article uses the narrow common denominator: prohibited-item screening by default, with an optional visible/basic check where the live order offers it. It does not promise professional, functional or authenticity testing.
- No promotional purchase speed, shipping speed, storage period, fixed fee, market-price comparison or compensation outcome was published.
- No customer review, private order, destination-country rule or test purchase was used.
- Published copy contains no LoloBuy, seller, marketplace, carrier, social, review or source links. Its only rendered external link is to `https://findspreadsheet.com/`.
