## Editorial limitations

- The article covers shopping-agent orders before warehouse availability, not international parcel tracking.
- Official pages use both “In Stock” and “Stored in warehouse”; the article preserves both terms and explains the practical warehouse-availability test.
- Exact processing times, seller dispatch estimates, domestic delivery estimates and warehouse location were omitted.
- LoloBuy public pages conflict on free-storage duration (90 versus 180 days); no storage-day claim was published.
- A status label does not prove authenticity, seller stock, inspection success, return eligibility or international-route availability.
- No customer review, private order, destination-country rule, price test or test purchase was used.
- Published copy contains no LoloBuy, seller, carrier, marketplace, social, review or source links. Its only rendered external link is to `https://findspreadsheet.com/`.
