## Editorial limitations

- No private order, customer anecdote, live price, fee quote, seller decision, refund result or payment-method withdrawal claim was used.
- LoloBuy public help pages contain different timing statements for parcel after-sales contexts. The article therefore tells readers to use the deadline on the live parcel record and act promptly rather than publishing one universal post-delivery deadline.
- Later-stage refund timing is presented as seller-dependent, not guaranteed. The article does not promise reimbursement of freight, service charges, currency differences or international shipping.
- Published copy intentionally contains no LoloBuy, seller, marketplace, carrier, social or source links. Its only rendered external link is to `https://findspreadsheet.com/`.
