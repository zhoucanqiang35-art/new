## Editorial limitations

- This is an evidence checklist, not legal, tax or customs-broker advice.
- The article does not publish a US or European duty-free threshold, tax rate or route-specific declaration amount.
- It rejects under-declaration, gift mislabelling, vague descriptions and parcel splitting for tax avoidance.
- A correct declaration does not guarantee clearance, no tax, no inspection, no delay or route eligibility.
- Destination-country and commodity-specific requirements must be checked with the current authority, carrier or qualified professional.
- No customer review, private order, test parcel, tax outcome or clearance result was used.
- Published copy contains no LoloBuy, government, carrier, marketplace, social, review or source links. Its only rendered external link is to `https://findspreadsheet.com/`.
