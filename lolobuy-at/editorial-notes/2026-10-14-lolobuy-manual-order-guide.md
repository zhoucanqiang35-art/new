## Editorial limitations

- The public help article confirms a manual form but does not publish a definitive current list of interface field labels. The article presents an evidence checklist, not a claimed screenshot or exact UI specification; the live form controls required fields.
- LoloBuy public pages currently conflict on free-storage duration. Storage days are outside this article’s search intent and were omitted.
- Seller promotions, stock, prices, purchase eligibility and domestic delivery amounts are treated as facts requiring agent confirmation, not guaranteed outcomes.
- The guide does not state that a manually submitted item is authentic, legal, in stock, supported by a route or covered by a return right.
- No customer review, private order, destination-country rule, price test or test purchase was used.
- Published copy contains no LoloBuy, seller, marketplace, carrier, social, review or source links. Its only rendered external link is to `https://findspreadsheet.com/`.
