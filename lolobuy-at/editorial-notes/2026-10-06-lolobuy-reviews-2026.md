## Independent public evidence consulted

1. Trustpilot — LoloBuy profile
   `https://ca.trustpilot.com/review/lolobuy.com`

   Snapshot used: 42 reviews, 4.1 TrustScore, 67% five-star, 9% four-star, 10% three-star, 2% two-star and 12% one-star. The profile was claimed, showed a paid Trustpilot subscription, stated that the company asks customers for reviews and displayed invited, verified and unprompted labels. A cached page from two days earlier showed 36 reviews, confirming that the count is fast-changing; all figures are dated snapshots, not permanent facts.

   `https://apps.apple.com/us/app/lolobuy-shop-from-china/id6784489956`

   `https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?hl=en-US&id=com.hillian.lolobuy`

   Snapshot used: 1K+ downloads and developer identity. The retrieved page did not expose a usable aggregate rating, and downloads were not treated as completed purchases or endorsements.

4. Reddit — “[Preliminary Thoughts] My Experience with LoloBuy”
   `https://www.reddit.com/r/repweidiansneakers/comments/1u70yls/preliminary_thoughts_my_experience_with_lolobuy/`

## Editorial limitations

- No review was independently verified by this site, and no customer claim was presented as a confirmed platform fact.
- “Verified” and “invited” are source-platform labels, not this site’s validation of every statement in a review.
- The article does not calculate a success rate, average delivery time, fraud probability or universal safety/reliability verdict.
- Review themes were mapped to official workflow stages so seller, warehouse, platform support, carrier and customs responsibilities were not collapsed into one score.
- No private conversation, customer screenshot, testimonial, order data, live price or test purchase was used.
- Published copy intentionally contains no LoloBuy, Trustpilot, Apple, Google, Reddit, seller, carrier, social or source links. Its only rendered external link is to `https://findspreadsheet.com/`.
