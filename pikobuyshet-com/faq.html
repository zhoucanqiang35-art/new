Practical PikoBuy questions, answered from public platform information

This page summarises what PikoBuy publicly says about its workflow. It is independent research, not customer support or a guarantee of a particular order outcome.

01

What does PikoBuy say it does?

PikoBuy describes a purchasing and forwarding workflow: users can search for products or submit links, images or sourcing requests; orders are purchased and received at a China warehouse; items are inspected and repacked; then an international parcel can be submitted. The exact service available can depend on the item, seller, destination and current rules.

02

Can I submit a product link instead of searching?

Yes. PikoBuy’s public home page says users may submit a product link, image or sourcing request. Its beginner guide says users can paste a product link or keywords into the search box. Save the precise size, colour, quantity and seller information before submitting, because a link alone does not identify every variant.

03

Is the displayed item price the final amount I will pay?

Not necessarily. PikoBuy’s guide says prices are subject to actual purchase and that out-of-stock orders are refunded. International orders can also involve seller shipping, return fees where applicable, parcel weight or dimensions, international shipping and destination-country taxes or customs charges. Check the live order and shipping estimate instead of relying on a previous price.

04

What happens when an item reaches the warehouse?

PikoBuy says it performs check-in, photo confirmation and a defect check. Its shipping terms say forwarded goods are unpacked and inspected, and users can review inspection photos or purchase additional detailed photos. This can help identify visible issues, but it does not prove authenticity, product performance, fit or future delivery.

05

Can I request special packing?

PikoBuy’s beginner guide says users can add requests such as minimal packaging or reinforced packaging. Whether a request is possible and any cost should be checked in the live account flow. Packing requests should not be treated as a guarantee that a parcel will meet airline, carrier or customs requirements.

06

How is international shipping priced?

PikoBuy provides an estimate tool that asks for destination, product type, weight and parcel dimensions. Its guide says routes can differ in delivery time and billing methods. The final charge can change after warehouse receipt and parcel preparation, so treat an early calculation as an estimate rather than a fixed quote.

07

When does tracking become available?

PikoBuy’s public guide says tracking information will be available within three days after a parcel is shipped. This is not a delivery guarantee. Carrier handovers, route changes and destination processing can affect when a scan appears and how quickly a parcel moves.

08

Does PikoBuy guarantee customs clearance or delivery?

No. Its shipping terms say parcels are carried by third-party logistics providers and that cross-border risks include customs policy changes and uncontrollable events such as damage, loss, seizure and peak-season delays. Buyers should check current local import rules and budget for cross-border uncertainty before ordering.

09

Can an item be returned after it enters the warehouse?

PikoBuy’s returns page says a qualifying item must be covered by the seller’s return guarantee, remain in the seller-required resalable condition and have been in the warehouse for no more than five days. The return request must be submitted within five days after the status becomes “In Warehouse.” Seller rules and consent still affect the final outcome.

10

Which items may be ineligible for the return service?

The public policy lists examples including customised items, second-hand goods, intimate apparel, food, some products from outside mainland China and products whose required seals, labels, accessories or packaging have been removed. Always check the live policy and keep return-sensitive packaging intact until you decide.

11

Who pays for a return?

PikoBuy says a customer-initiated, no-fault return can include return shipping, the seller’s original shipping fee and a RMB 5 service fee. Where a seller is responsible for a wrong item or verified quality issue, the policy describes a different cost outcome, subject to the actual case and seller response. Review current policy details before submitting a request.

Research note

Keep the decision current

These answers are based on PikoBuy’s publicly available Home, Beginner’s Guide, Shipping Terms, Shipping Estimate and Returns & Exchanges pages, checked in September 2026. Policies can change.
