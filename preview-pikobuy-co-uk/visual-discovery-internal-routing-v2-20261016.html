FAQ

Questions before you open a product.

Clear context for browsing the local catalog, product imagery and the handoff to PikoBuy.

How are the prices shown on this board?

Catalog prices are displayed in USD by converting the recorded CNY amount at 6.69 CNY per USD. This gives a consistent way to compare preview records, but it is not a checkout quote. The original seller price, selected variant, domestic shipping and exchange rate can change before an order is processed, so the official PikoBuy page is the place to confirm the live amount.

Which images appear on a product detail page?

Every product page follows the same image order: the title, the product’s Weidian main image and switchable Weidian original-image thumbnails appear first. The continuous image stream immediately below is reserved for that product’s QC original photos, so source images and inspection evidence stay visually separate while remaining in one uninterrupted browse.

What happens when a product has no QC images?

Only when a record has no exported QC photos, the lower QC stream falls back to that same product’s Weidian images. The fallback is labelled in the page so it cannot be mistaken for warehouse QC. Images from another product are never used to fill an empty record.

Does a product record confirm live stock or final options?

No. This board is for discovery and comparison. A record does not confirm seller stock, the final colour or size, authenticity, delivery timing, duty or a final total. Before paying, check the original listing and then confirm the selected option, price and current terms on the official PikoBuy product page.

Article 01 · source-first workflow

How to use PikoBuy for Taobao, Weidian and 1688

Start with the original listing rather than a spreadsheet card: save the URL, title, seller images, exact option name, measurements and stated domestic delivery terms. This makes the item you want clear before any agent order is placed.

Treat the purchase, warehouse check and international parcel as separate decisions. When an item reaches the warehouse, compare visible colour, tags, piece count and obvious condition against the source listing. Submit an international parcel only after you know what you are keeping and have reviewed the shipping estimate for the destination.

Article 02 · QC, returns and shipping

How to read QC photos without guessing

QC photos are useful for visible checks, not a guarantee of authenticity, internal function, future condition or delivery outcome. Use them for concrete comparisons: left and right pair consistency, label placement, colour, measurements, stitching, accessory count and obvious marks.

If a decision depends on one detail, request or review a targeted close-up instead of assuming a wide photo answers it. Keep return and parcel decisions separate: the available route, billed weight, dimensions, duties and carrier timing need to be confirmed for the actual parcel and destination.
