04 / Field notes

How to use a PikoBuy spreadsheet.

Discovery data is useful only when its limits are visible. Treat it as a research map—not proof of stock, quality, authenticity, shipping or final pricing.

Search with an intent

Start with a product type, style cue or item ID. This keeps the first comparison focused and makes it easier to tell records apart.

Read a record before a listing

Compare category, USD reference price, item ID and QC-reference count inside the subsite. Then decide whether the live item is worth opening.

Verify at the last step

Before an order, use the official PikoBuy product page to confirm the selected option, price, terms and any relevant destination restrictions.
