PikoBuy buying guide

What a PikoBuy spreadsheet is actually for.

A pikobuy spreadsheet is a faster product-discovery format for people who would otherwise hunt through scattered Taobao, Weidian and 1688 links. A good record gives you enough context to decide whether opening the source item is worthwhile: a clear category, a reference price in USD, a product ID, source clues and any available QC-photo references.

Use it as a research map, not a guarantee. Search by a practical intent such as “hoodie”, “sneaker” or an item ID; compare the record with the current source listing; then confirm the selected variation, size chart, price, destination restrictions and the provider’s own terms. A product page is the final point to review before you order.

For pikobuy qc photos, the right question is not “does this prove the product is good?” but “what visible details does this particular photo document?” That keeps the decision grounded in the evidence that is actually available.

What is the difference between a PikoBuy spreadsheet and the official PikoBuy site?

This independent preview organises discovery records. The official PikoBuy site handles its own account, product-page and service experience.

Why does this preview label data as preview data?

There is not yet a verified live data connection to FindSpreadsheet. The label prevents reference records from being mistaken for current product, stock, price or QC information.

How does an item button work?

It passes the product ID into the official PikoBuy product-detail URL. Review the destination and selected item details there.
