Is this an official Joyagoo website?

No. It is an independent research preview and it does not process accounts, payments, orders, warehouse actions or shipping.

Where do the buttons go?

All category, search and research exits go only to FindSpreadsheet.

What does public Joyagoo guidance describe?

It describes warehouse QC photos after arrival, a possible 5-day request period after QC for eligible items, 90 days of free item storage after Stored, and parcel submission before international shipping. Conditions can change.

Does this site promise quality or delivery?

No. Check current records and the official platform terms before acting.
