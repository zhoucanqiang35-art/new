06 / FAQ • fact check

Useful answers, without sales talk.

These answers follow Joyagoo’s public shopping, QC, fee and logistics guidance. Where a policy or route can change, the answer tells you to check the live account screen.

Independent site: Joyagoo Sheet is not Joyagoo. It does not process orders, hold products or give a delivery guarantee.

Can I use a product link to start an order?

Joyagoo’s public shopping guidance says a buyer can paste an original product link into its search field. It also describes searching by product name or photo. Before you submit, confirm the exact colour, size and quantity rather than relying on the product title alone.

Are the displayed products sold by Joyagoo itself?

No. Joyagoo’s purchasing instructions say items shown on its shopping-agent pages and in product search results come from third-party shopping platforms. Treat seller images and descriptions as seller-provided information, then use QC photos to review the received item.

What happens when an item reaches the warehouse?

Joyagoo describes a warehouse-arrival stage followed by quality control and product photographs. Its public materials say these photos help the buyer get a general view of the item. Review them quickly and compare them with the variation you selected.

Does QC prove that an item is perfect or authentic?

No. QC photos are visual evidence, not a blanket guarantee. Joyagoo notes that colour can vary because of environment and equipment, and its QC guidance says some details cannot be guaranteed with 100% accuracy. Look for major mismatches or defects; request a targeted close-up when the existing photo cannot answer a specific question.

Can I ask for more detailed photos?

Yes. Joyagoo publicly describes a detailed-photo service for requested angles, close-ups and measurements after warehouse arrival. It says standard photos follow warehouse standards, while detailed photos can be tailored to specific requirements. Check the current fee and limits in the live service page before ordering.

How long can items remain in the warehouse?

Joyagoo’s shopping guidance currently states 90 days of free warehouse storage per product and describes a paid extension option. Because storage rules can change, verify the current storage display and policy in your account before waiting to consolidate a parcel.

Is the international shipping fee final when I submit a parcel?

Not necessarily. The platform’s shopping guidance says a normal parcel submission begins with an estimated charge, while the final fee is determined after packing from the actual size and weight. It also describes rehearsal packing as an alternative for learning the packed result before the final shipping payment.

Why are some shipping lines unavailable for my item?

Joyagoo says some products may have delivery-method restrictions and that its system screens the shipping lines available for the products in a parcel. Use the options actually shown for your destination; do not assume a line used for a previous parcel will be available again.

What costs should I expect besides the item price?

Joyagoo’s fee explanation lists domestic shipping from seller to warehouse, international parcel shipping and possible customs charges. It says international charges depend on factors such as weight, volume, method and destination. Returns, extra photos and other services may also have their own current fees.

How do I check a parcel after dispatch?

Joyagoo directs users to the Parcel area and its Tracking Details after a parcel is sent. Its logistics guidance warns that international routing can include multiple transits and that tracking updates may lag. Check the live status and the route’s current estimate before treating a gap as a final outcome.

Can shipping be guaranteed to a particular country or date?

No. The public shipping guidance identifies risks including delays, taxes, customs handling, damage and loss during third-party logistics or customs processes. An estimate is useful for planning, but it is not a delivery promise. Choose your route using the current destination-specific information.

Where can I browse the independent product database?

The category and product pages on this site link only to the main database. Use them to discover product types, then make your own verification of availability, price, sizing and platform policy before any purchase decision.
