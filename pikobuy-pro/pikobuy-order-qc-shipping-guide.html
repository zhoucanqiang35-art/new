A practical buyer checklist

Use this checklist before moving from a research page to a live product search:

  • Find the current item listing and selected option.
  • Check price, measurements, seller photos and product restrictions.
  • Save the order details before first payment.
  • Review warehouse photos for visible issues as soon as they are available.
  • Check return eligibility and the warehouse deadline before changing packaging or submitting a parcel.
  • Use current parcel weight and dimensions when comparing shipping routes.
  • Wait for the available tracking information rather than assuming a fixed delivery date.
