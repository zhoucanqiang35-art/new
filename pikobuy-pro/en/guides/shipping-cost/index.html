Actual and volumetric weight

Some routes charge based on the greater of actual and volumetric weight. For example, 40 × 30 × 20 cm divided by 6,000 is 4 kg; the divisor varies by carrier, so this is an illustration, not a quote. Packaging changes both protection and dimensions.

Compare the final parcel

Once items reach the warehouse, use measured weight and dimensions to compare available routes, delivery ranges and insurance exclusions. Review the complete charge at checkout; estimates and route availability can change.
