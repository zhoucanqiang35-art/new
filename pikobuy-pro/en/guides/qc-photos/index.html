Compare the received item with the exact order: model, colour, size label, quantity and included accessories. Inspect both sides under adequate lighting and request a close-up if the photo hides a decisive detail.

Measure and decide

For clothing, compare chest width and length with a garment that fits. For shoes, request an insole measurement where useful; compare the measurement method rather than relying only on the printed size. Record stains, seams and hardware.

Act before the deadline

Check the current seller and platform return rules as soon as the item is warehoused. Photos can show visible defects but cannot authenticate an item or prove durability, fit or material composition. Ask for additional photos before accepting or submitting the parcel.
