PikoBuy Order Tracking: Status, Delays & Delivery

An order status concerns the seller’s dispatch and warehouse arrival; a parcel status begins after you submit the international shipment. Keep the order number and parcel tracking number separate. A label created before carrier pickup does not mean the parcel is already moving.

When tracking does not update

Check the tracking number on the carrier’s own site, the date of the last scan, and whether the parcel has actually been handed over. Some routes have gaps between scans. If the delay exceeds the current route’s stated range, contact PikoBuy support with the parcel number and screenshots.

Before and after delivery

Check your address before submission and save the tracking history. If delivery is marked complete but the parcel is missing, check the carrier’s proof and local delivery arrangements, then contact the carrier and platform promptly. Tracking is not a guaranteed delivery date.
