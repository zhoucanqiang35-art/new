FAQ

Practical PikoBuy questions before you commit to a product or parcel.

These answers summarize current public PikoBuy help pages. Re-check live seller, route and checkout rules when you are ready to act.

What does PikoBuy say its warehouse inspection includes?

Its beginner guide describes check-in, photo confirmation and a defect check after items reach the warehouse. Use those photos to compare the received item to your selected variant; do not treat a basic photo as proof of specialist performance.

Can I consolidate purchases from different sellers?

PikoBuy says it centrally manages orders from different sources. Decide only after each item is received and reviewed. Combine goods because the route and packing make sense, not merely because they arrived on the same day.

How should I use PikoBuy’s shipping estimate?

Its estimator asks for destination, product type, weight and dimensions. Use realistic outer parcel assumptions and compare the same parcel across routes. The result is a planning estimate, not a guaranteed final freight charge.

Does PikoBuy say when tracking appears?

Its beginner guide says tracking information is available within three days after shipment. Save the parcel number, route and submission time, then assess an update against that stated window before escalating.

Who handles customs risk?

PikoBuy’s shipping terms say third-party logistics providers carry international parcels and that customs policies and uncontrollable cross-border events remain a risk. Check the live route restrictions and destination requirements before paying.

What is the PikoBuy return window described on its site?

The returns page describes eligible requests within five days, stated as 120 hours, after an order changes to In Warehouse. Eligibility also depends on seller rules, resale condition and category restrictions.

Are all goods eligible for return?

No. PikoBuy lists examples such as customized items, second-hand goods, intimate apparel, food and some opened or sealed products as not returnable. Seller consent is also required for a return.

What evidence helps with an after-sales request?

Keep the product listing, chosen variant, warehouse photos, issue description, order status and relevant timestamps together. One concise comparison between what was ordered and what arrived is more useful than a broad complaint.

Can the site guarantee shipping cost, delivery time or customs clearance?

No. Routes, packing, carrier handling and destination rules can change. Confirm the checkout details for the exact product and destination before submitting a parcel.

Does this site sell products?

No. It is an independent research and browsing guide. Product and search actions take you to FindsSpreadsheet.
