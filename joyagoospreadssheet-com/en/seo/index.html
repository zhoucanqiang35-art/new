How to Use a Joyagoo Spreadsheet: Find, Check and Order Products

A spreadsheet is a discovery index. Check the live product listing and current options before paying.

Find a candidate

Search a product or use the matching category. Record the item link, seller, price, chosen colour, size and quantity. A listing may change or disappear, so save the information you used to choose. FindSpreadsheet is the product database; this independent guide does not process orders.

Order and check the item

Joyagoo’s published guidance describes searching with a product link or name, selecting the option and paying for the product and domestic shipping. Once the warehouse receives the item, inspect the available QC photos against the order. Ask a specific question about any missing view or measurement and confirm optional service costs.

Decide before submitting a parcel

If the item is wrong or visibly damaged, contact the agent through the order promptly. Joyagoo describes a return or exchange request within five days after QC completion; check current eligibility and conditions. Once each item is resolved, review available routes, packing, the international shipping quote and destination requirements before payment.

Treat the spreadsheet as a starting point

A discovery index collects links and context; it is not a guarantee that a seller will keep the same price, sizes or stock. Open the current product record and compare the available options with what you saved. A result can point to a changed listing, a different colour or a size chart with different units. If the detail that made the item suitable is no longer visible, do not assume that the old record still describes it.

Compare useful details before price

For shoes, look at the seller’s size information and a measurement from a pair that fits. For clothing, compare chest, length and other relevant measurements with a garment measured in the same way. For electronics, check compatibility, plug, model and restrictions. A low listing price alone does not tell you whether an item fits, works or is economical to send.

Understand two payment stages

Joyagoo’s guidance describes paying for the product and domestic delivery first. International freight is a later parcel decision after warehouse arrival. Keep these amounts separate, along with any paid extra photos, packing services and potential destination charges. The final amount depends on the chosen goods, parcel and current checkout; another buyer’s total is not your quote.

Read warehouse photos against the order

Compare the exact variant, quantity and visible condition with the confirmation. Ask for a useful view or measurement when it can change your decision, and check whether it costs extra. Joyagoo says its electronics check is visual and does not include a power-on test. Photos are not proof of authenticity, unseen materials, performance or future condition.

Make a timely return decision

If a discrepancy appears, use the order’s contact channel and explain the selected option, visible difference and requested remedy. Joyagoo’s guide describes requesting a return or exchange within five days after QC completion. Check the current order, seller conditions, item state and possible costs; a request is not a guaranteed refund. Resolve it before submitting the item in an international parcel.

Plan packing and keep a record

After each item is resolved, compare the available destination routes and product restrictions. Consider protection, boxes, package size and any optional services, then check the final quote before paying. Save the order, photos, route, parcel number and dated tracking events. Follow destination rules and provide accurate information; no route guarantees customs clearance or a delivery date.

A saved find is a starting point

A product card can help you shortlist an item, but its seller, variants, price or stock may change. Open the current product record and compare its identifier and selected options with your notes. If the listing has disappeared or changed, verify the replacement before ordering.

Keep each payment decision separate

Before ordering, confirm product price, domestic delivery and the chosen variant. After warehouse arrival, assess QC and any return deadline. Only then compare the international parcel quote, packing choices and destination charges. Additional services may have separate fees; check the current account rather than relying on an old estimate.

Keep a small evidence record

Save the order confirmation, selected option, relevant listing information, warehouse images and parcel identifier. If something is unclear, ask a specific question and retain the reply. This guide is independent; the relevant service controls the live order, policies and payment screens.
