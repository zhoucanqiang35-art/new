FAQ · ON THIS PAGE

Answers before the next step.

Open standalone page ↗
What is a Joyagoo spreadsheet?

It is a way to discover product listings. Check the live listing, selected option and current price before making a decision.

Are warehouse photos the same as seller photos?

No. Seller images advertise a listing; warehouse photos show the received item and only the visible details in the image.

Does the product price include international shipping?

Do not assume so. Check product, domestic delivery, optional services and international shipping separately before payment.

Is this the official Joyagoo website?

No. This is an independent product-research guide. Account, payment, warehouse and shipping actions take place on the relevant service.

When should I request a return or exchange?

Joyagoo’s shopping guide says requests can be made within five days after QC completion. Check the current order and act promptly; eligibility and terms can change.

What conditions apply to a return?

Check the current seller and Joyagoo conditions, the item’s state and the applicable deadline before requesting a return or exchange.

Which items are eligible for return?

Eligibility can depend on the seller, product type and condition. Check the live order and current rules; do not assume every item can be returned.

Who pays return costs?

Joyagoo says return shipping and handling charges may apply. Ask the agent for the current amount and conditions before confirming.

How long is warehouse storage?

Joyagoo’s published shopping guide describes 90 days of free storage per item and an extension option. Verify the current deadline and extension terms in your account.

When will tracking appear?

Tracking timing depends on the route and carrier. Check the parcel record and dated events; a tracking estimate is not a delivery guarantee.
