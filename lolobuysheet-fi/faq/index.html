Research FAQ

Clear, evidence-led answers before you use a product link.

This independent guide uses LoloBuy’s public link-based purchase-assistance description as its starting point and does not invent platform policies, prices, delivery promises or quality claims.

What does LoloBuy publicly say it does?

Its public registration page says it assists with purchasing products from online and offline channels in China, and that a user can paste a product link to begin an order. This is a description of a purchase-assistance starting point, not a promise about every later service, price or outcome.

Is this an official LoloBuy website?

No. LoloBuy Sheet.fi is an independent research preview. It does not represent LoloBuy, process orders, set platform terms or provide LoloBuy customer service.

Why should I check a product listing before using a link?

A link can contain several options, images and prices. Check the exact item, selected size or colour, visible details and price context first. This reduces the risk of relying on a title or image that refers to a different variation.

Does a product image prove quality or authenticity?

No. Images can help compare visible details such as a measurement, colour block, label or hardware shape. They cannot guarantee quality, authenticity, durability, seller reliability or later delivery performance.

Does this guide claim that every order receives QC photos?

No. This guide does not claim a universal QC or warehouse-image policy. If an active process provides an image or an inspection option, use it as visible evidence for that item and check the current terms shown at that time.

Can this site promise shipping time, taxes or customs results?

No. Shipping options, costs, restrictions, tax treatment and customs outcomes depend on the current parcel, destination and live terms. Review those conditions when you are ready to act, and consult official destination guidance where needed.

Where do the database and product links go?

All database buttons go directly to the FindSpreadsheet homepage. Category-detail buttons go only to their matching FindSpreadsheet category page. This preview does not link visitors to unrelated agents or third-party directories.

What happens when I use the search box?

With a keyword, it opens the FindSpreadsheet search result for that term. With an empty search, it opens the FindSpreadsheet homepage. The search box does not take payment or place an order.

Why are there nine language choices?

The site is designed for an English-led European and US audience, with English, German, French, Spanish, Italian, Portuguese, Dutch, Polish and Swedish language choices. The language list is audience targeting, not a claim about LoloBuy customer numbers by country.

What is the safest way to use this research?

Keep a short note with the current product link, exact selected option, visible reference image and the one or two questions that matter. Then review the current information in the active purchase flow instead of relying on an old post, screenshot or generic promise.
