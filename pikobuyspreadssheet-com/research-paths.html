Research paths

Compare, plan and verify before a database search.

Use these three research paths to review the item context, compare similar options and plan the final parcel decision.

01

Product link research

Confirm the exact variant, sizing context and source page before comparing an item.

02

Category comparison

Compare similar items together so missing sizing, material or photo information is easier to spot.

03

Parcel planning

Check packed weight, route and timing before moving an item into a serious shortlist.

Official-flow research checks

Choose and search

Confirm colour, size and specifications, then search with the product link or keywords.

Order and warehouse

Treat purchasing, warehouse arrival, inspection and available photos as separate checks.

Ship and track

Compare route, packed weight and destination factors before final shipping decisions.

Regions and destination checks

Research is written for Europe and North America. Carrier availability, tax and customs conditions can change, so confirm the current destination information before paying.
