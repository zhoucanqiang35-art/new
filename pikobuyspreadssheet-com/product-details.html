Product details

Product research before opening the database.

Use the detail page to check the current title, selected variant, measurements, photo set and likely packed weight. Then search the live FindsSpreadsheet database with the exact category or product name.

01

Read the item context

Confirm the product type, colour, size and material notes before treating a listed image as a match.

02

Check evidence

Use photos and measurements appropriate for the category. Images help inspection but do not guarantee stock or quality.

03

Search the live database

When the row still makes sense, open the matching FindSpreadsheet search results and review the current product detail.
