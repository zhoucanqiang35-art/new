FAQ

Clear answers before an order needs a live, account-specific answer.

These answers use LoloBuy’s public app descriptions for platform-level facts. Product availability, prices, payment choices and delivery options must be verified in the live flow for the exact order.

  1. 01

    What is LoloBuy?

    LoloBuy’s public app descriptions present it as a cross-border e-commerce and shopping service for consumers and merchants. The descriptions say users can discover and purchase Chinese products; they do not act as a catalogue of every product that is currently available.

  2. 02

    Does LoloBuy publicly mention international shipping?

    Yes. International shipping is included in the published app description. That is a platform-level feature statement, not a guarantee that every item, destination, parcel size or service level will be available for your particular order.

  3. 03

    Does it support more than one language or currency?

    The public app descriptions mention multi-language and multi-currency support. They do not publish a universal list of every language, currency, exchange rate, fee or regional rule. Check what your own product and checkout screens display.

  4. 04

    Does “secure payments” tell me which payment methods I can use?

    No. The public description uses the phrase “secure payments,” but it does not publish a complete payment-method or eligibility list. Treat the methods and total shown to your account at checkout as the decision information.

  5. 05

    Can this site tell me whether a product is in stock?

    No. A discovery page, old screenshot or saved link cannot prove live stock. Reopen the exact listing and selected option before paying. If stock or a variant is not clearly shown, do not assume it is available.

  6. 06

    Can this site promise a shipping price or arrival date?

    No. LoloBuy’s public materials do not provide a universal route table, carrier list, price or delivery-time promise. The live order flow is the correct place to inspect the options shown for your destination and order.

  7. 07

    How should I use a product link safely?

    Use it as the beginning of research, not the final decision. Record the title, exact option, current price, stated material or specification and visible photos. Then check the live listing again before you continue.

  8. 08

    Why do size, colour and configuration need separate attention?

    They can change the item itself or its current price. The headline image may not show the option you select. Read the active variant, the resulting price and the seller’s stated information as one record.

  9. 09

    What does FindSpreadsheet do in this process?

    FindSpreadsheet is the product-discovery destination linked from this site. It does not process a LoloBuy payment or make an account-specific delivery decision. Those facts belong to the live platform flow.

  10. 10

    What does this guide deliberately not claim?

    It does not claim fixed delivery times, country-by-country availability, carrier choices, customs outcomes, payment methods, exchange rates, stock levels, seller quality or guaranteed savings. Those claims would need current, order-specific evidence.
