FAQ

Clear boundaries for research

This is an independent guide. Use the live official order flow for account, payment, shipping, return and tracking decisions.

What does USFans publicly describe itself as?

USFans publicly presents a China buying-agent workflow for marketplace sourcing, warehouse photo confirmation and international shipping. This guide is independent and is not an official USFans service.

Is a spreadsheet or directory a store?

No. A directory is a research layer that can help a visitor discover and compare listings. Product stock, variations, seller terms and displayed prices must be verified on the live product page.

What are QC photos useful for?

QC photos can show visible warehouse evidence such as item type, selected variation, labels and obvious surface details. They cannot guarantee authenticity, long-term durability, material composition or a particular fit.

Does QC confirm that every product is perfect?

No. Visible evidence is helpful, but it is not a certification. Review the exact views that matter to you and request clarification before submitting a parcel when a missing angle could change your decision.

Can an item be returned after warehouse arrival?

Eligibility depends on the seller, item condition, domestic shipping and current order terms. Review the live order information promptly instead of treating any historic return window as a universal guarantee.

Why can the final cost differ from the product price?

Domestic shipping, selected options, packaging, parcel dimensions, actual or chargeable weight, route availability and destination requirements can affect the total cost.

Does global shipping mean delivery is guaranteed?

No. Route availability, carrier handling and customs requirements vary. A guide cannot promise delivery dates, duties, clearance or a shipping price.

Where do this site’s category and product buttons go?

Every external research button on this site goes only to FindSpreadsheet, where current product discovery can continue.
