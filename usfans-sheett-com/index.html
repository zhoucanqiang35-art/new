FAQ

Research with clear boundaries.

What does USFans publicly describe itself as?

USFans publicly presents a China buying-agent workflow for marketplace sourcing, warehouse photo confirmation and international shipping. This independent site is not an official USFans service.

What are QC photos useful for?

They are visible evidence of an item at a point in the warehouse process. They can help you check the selected variant, labels and visible details, but they are not a certification of fit, authenticity or durability.

Why can the final cost differ from the product price?

Domestic shipping, options, parcel size, chargeable weight, route availability and destination rules can affect the total. Check each current order screen before paying.
