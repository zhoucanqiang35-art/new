FACT-BASED FAQ

Useful answers.
Clear limits.

Answers are based on PikoBuy's public home page, beginner guide, shipping terms and returns page, reviewed 7 September 2026. Rules and availability can change, so confirm live details before ordering.

What does PikoBuy say it does?

PikoBuy describes itself as a China purchasing and forwarding platform. Its public pages describe product discovery, buy-for-me orders, warehouse inspection, repacking and international shipping from its China warehouse.

Can I submit a product link or image?

Yes. PikoBuy says shoppers can search directly on the platform or submit product links, images or sourcing requests so that product details can be confirmed and the purchasing process can proceed.

What should I confirm before submitting an order?

Confirm the product specification, colour, size and quantity. PikoBuy's guide recommends selecting these details before the first payment. Saving the original listing and store information also makes later checking easier.

Is the item price guaranteed at the time I see it?

No fixed-price guarantee should be assumed. PikoBuy's guide says prices are subject to the actual purchase and that an out-of-stock order will be refunded. Check the current item and keep room in your budget for changes.

What happens when an item arrives at the warehouse?

PikoBuy says items receive quality inspection and repacking at its China warehouse. Its guide describes check-in, photo confirmation and defect checking before an international parcel is submitted.

Can warehouse photos guarantee quality or authenticity?

No. Photos can help confirm visible details such as the received variation, size label, colour, obvious damage or included pieces. They cannot guarantee authenticity, fit, materials not visible in the image, long-term durability or delivery outcome.

Can I ask for different packaging?

PikoBuy's beginner guide says customers can add requests such as minimal packaging or reinforced packaging at the warehouse stage. Whether a request is suitable depends on the item and the available service.

How does PikoBuy estimate international shipping?

Its estimate form asks for destination country or region, product type, weight, length, width and height. A quote therefore depends on the actual parcel and route rather than on one universal shipping price.

When does tracking appear?

PikoBuy's guide says tracking information will be available within three days after a parcel is shipped. This describes the tracking handoff, not a guaranteed delivery date.

Does PikoBuy control every delivery risk?

No. Its shipping terms say parcels are carried by third-party logistics providers and list cross-border risks including customs policies, damage, loss, confiscation and peak-season delays. Build schedule and budget margin into any international order.

What is the published warehouse return window?

For items that qualify under the seller and platform rules, PikoBuy's returns page says an application can be made within five days after the status becomes “In Warehouse,” counted as 120 hours. Eligibility, product condition and seller rules still matter.

Are all products eligible for a return?

No. PikoBuy lists exclusions and conditions, including examples such as customised items, second-hand products, intimate apparel and products that no longer meet resale requirements. Check the current live policy for the specific item before changing tags or packaging.

Is PikoBuySpread the official PikoBuy site?

No. PikoBuySpread is an independent research preview. It does not process payments, orders, inspection requests, returns or shipping. Its product-discovery buttons direct to FindSpreadsheet only.
