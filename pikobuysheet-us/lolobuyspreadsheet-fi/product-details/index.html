Product research

Product details to check before opening an agent route.

A product card is a starting point. Compare the actual listing information before relying on an old price, a visual summary or a seller description.

01 · listing

Match the product and option

Check the product version, selected size or colour, stated measurements, and the listing image set. Similar names can describe different variants.

02 · QC

Write a specific inspection question

Choose visible details that matter for the item: measurements, stitching, colour, hardware or packaging. QC photos are useful evidence, not a quality guarantee.

03 · parcel

Choose shipping later

Parcel weight, warehouse availability, destination rules and insurance options should be checked with current checkout information.
