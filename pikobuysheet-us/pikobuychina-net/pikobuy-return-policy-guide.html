PikoBuy Return Policy Guide: The 120-Hour Warehouse Window

A return is not automatic just because an item is still in China. This practical guide explains the timing, seller rules, evidence and possible costs to check before submitting an international parcel.

Independent research · reviewed 7 September 2026

Buying through a China purchasing agent creates a short but important decision period. The original seller sends an item to the agent’s warehouse, warehouse photos become available, and you decide whether the received item should move into an international parcel. If the size, colour, quantity or visible condition appears wrong, this warehouse stage is the time to compare evidence and study the return rules. Waiting until international dispatch can turn a seller-side question into a much more complicated cross-border problem.

PikoBuy’s current Returns & Exchanges page describes a “5-Day Return Guarantee,” but that label should not be read as a universal promise. The page says this after-sales service is provided by third-party platform sellers, and it ties eligibility to the seller’s return guarantee, the item’s resale condition and the time spent in the warehouse. The practical lesson is simple: check the exact product and seller terms, watch the warehouse status, and act on evidence rather than assuming every order can be sent back.

What the five-day wording actually means

PikoBuy states that a return request can be submitted within five days after the order status changes to “In Warehouse.” Its page explains that the count begins from the next hour after that status change and that five days equal 120 hours. It also warns that applications may not be accepted after this period. Record the status time when the warehouse entry appears; do not calculate from the day you happen to open the photos or from the day you plan to ship.

The same policy describes the broader seller service as applying within seven days after signing for the product, expressed on the page as within five days of warehouse entry. For an international buyer, the clearest actionable marker is the order status and the stated 120-hour application window. Check the live account record because this article cannot see or determine the timestamp for an individual order.

Eligibility depends on more than timing

Submitting quickly is necessary, but it is not the only condition. PikoBuy says a returnable item must meet all of the following: the seller offers a return guarantee, the product remains in the resalable condition required by the seller, and it has been in the warehouse for no more than five days. The policy also says PikoBuy can return the product only with the seller’s consent. That makes seller approval and the original marketplace rules part of the result.

Before buying, save the source listing and look for its return position. After warehouse receipt, avoid requests that could remove packaging, labels or accessories until you have decided to keep the item. PikoBuy lists products whose packaging, labels or accessories were removed at the user’s request among items that may not qualify. A warehouse service request can therefore affect the later return decision.

Use warehouse evidence before making a claim

Start with the exact option on the paid order: product title, colour, size, quantity and included pieces. Compare it with the warehouse record and visible photographs. A different size label, wrong colour, missing piece, hole or stain can be documented with a saved listing screenshot and the relevant warehouse image. PikoBuy’s policy gives seller shipment of the wrong colour or size and product quality issues such as holes or stains as examples under seller responsibility.

A photograph still has limits. It cannot prove how something fits, whether an internal component works or whether every unseen area is flawless. Ask for a focused additional photo only when it can answer a specific visible question. The site’s PikoBuy QC photo guide provides a category-based method for that comparison. Describe the mismatch factually; avoid turning an uncertain impression into a claim the evidence cannot support.

Separate seller responsibility from buyer choice

The reason selected on a return request matters. PikoBuy’s current policy lists a change of mind, buying the wrong product or quantity, shipping cost exceeding the buyer’s budget, and a seller finding no quality problem as examples of customer responsibility. It separately lists a wrong product, a seller-side quality issue, or damage during domestic shipping under seller or other responsibility examples.

Do not choose a reason simply because it appears financially favourable. Match the reason to the records. If the seller sent the option you selected but you changed your mind, say so. If the visible size label does not match the paid order, provide both pieces of evidence. The policy says that for returns not caused by the customer, customers normally do not bear return costs, while noting an exception if the seller insists that the customer pay the shipping fee. That wording is not a guarantee that every dispute will be resolved without cost.

Understand the possible return charges

For an unconditional return, PikoBuy’s page gives the formula as shipping back to the seller, the seller’s original shipping fee, and an RMB 5 service fee. It adds that even when the item was advertised with free shipping at purchase, the first delivery charge may still need to be paid when requesting a return. For products without free shipping, the policy says the buyer bears both the seller-to-warehouse charge and the return shipment to the seller when the return is not based on a quality issue.

The policy also tells users to keep enough account balance to cover the return shipping fee and service fee after applying. Do not present the item price as the complete refund outcome. PikoBuy says the total refund is based on the actual product price paid, while return-related charges follow the reason and applicable rules. Check the figures shown for the individual request rather than copying an example into a general estimate.

Check exclusions and product-specific condition standards

PikoBuy’s not-returnable examples include customised products, second-hand products, certain products sourced outside mainland China, intimate apparel, food, periodicals and other goods excluded by the seller. Its table also contains special packaging and condition standards for several categories. These are not minor details: opening, removing or using an item can make resale impossible even while the time window remains open.

For new sneakers, the policy says an anti-theft buckle and tamper-proof sticker, if present, must remain uncut, attached and functional. For various sealed personal-care, cosmetic, food, software and related goods, the applicable disposable seal must remain intact. It lists additional standards for cameras, electronic components, books and other categories. Read the live table for the exact product rather than assuming that one example covers the whole catalogue.

Do not confuse purchase returns with forwarding after-sales

PikoBuy’s shipping terms make a separate point about forwarded goods: for after-sales service, the user should contact the sender or seller, while PikoBuy says it can help ship the goods back. This distinction matters when an item was sent to the warehouse through forwarding rather than purchased under the ordinary buying flow. The original sender’s or seller’s terms can control whether a remedy exists.

Keep the source, sender and order type clear in your records. If warehouse receipt shows something missing, PikoBuy’s shipping terms tell the user to contact the sender for verification and say the platform is not liable for missing items not caused by PikoBuy. A purchasing service, a forwarding service and a third-party seller do not necessarily carry the same obligations.

Make the return decision before parcel submission

PikoBuy’s beginner guide places warehouse inspection before route selection and international shipping payment. Follow that sequence. Do not combine an unresolved item into a parcel simply to keep the order moving. Once the item is sent internationally, seller approval, domestic return shipping and the warehouse time window are no longer the same practical path.

If the concern is that international shipping costs more than expected, compare the current parcel estimate before the return deadline. PikoBuy itself lists shipping cost exceeding the buyer’s budget as a customer-responsibility example. The site’s PikoBuy shipping guide explains why destination, product type, weight and dimensions should be reviewed early rather than after the return window has passed.

A defensible five-step return record

Keep one compact folder or note containing the original listing, selected option, payment record, “In Warehouse” timestamp, warehouse photos, return reason and messages. First confirm the deadline. Second check seller eligibility. Third preserve the item’s returnable condition. Fourth compare the paid option with visible evidence. Fifth submit the accurate reason and supporting images while the window is open.

This process cannot promise that a seller will accept a request, that a fee will be waived or that a refund will arrive on a particular date. It does make the decision clearer and easier to support. No customer review, private outcome or invented test was used.
