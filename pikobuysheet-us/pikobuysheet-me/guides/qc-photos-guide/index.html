PikoBuy QC Photos: What to Review

Warehouse photos are most useful when you compare them against the specific item details saved before ordering.

Start with visible, checkable details

Review product type, selected colour or variation, size label, quantity and any obvious visible damage. When an order contains a set, pair or accessory, make sure the photos provide enough information to identify the expected items.

Ask focused questions

If a photo does not answer a decision-changing question, request a precise view when that option is available. For example: a size label, a visible component, a packaging corner or the included accessory. A broad request to “check quality” is less likely to produce information you can compare.

Know the boundaries

A photo cannot prove authenticity, internal performance, long-term durability, exact material composition or a future customs result. Use it to identify visible errors before international shipping, then decide whether you need clarification, a return request or parcel submission.
