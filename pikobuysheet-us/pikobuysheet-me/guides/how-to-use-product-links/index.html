How to Use a PikoBuy Product Link

A product link is the starting record for an order. It should preserve the exact page and variation you selected—not only a product name or screenshot.

What to save before placing an order

Keep the original product link, seller or store name, selected size, colour, quantity and any listing details that influenced the decision. If a choice is important, record it before checkout. Product pages can change, and a generic title may not identify the exact variation later.

Why the original information matters

When an item reaches the warehouse, photos can be compared with the selected variation and visible listing details. This is most useful for clear questions: the wrong size label, an unexpected colour, a missing visible component or the wrong quantity. Photos are evidence of visible details; they do not prove material quality, authenticity or long-term performance.

Before parcel submission

Resolve visible mismatches while the item remains at the warehouse. Keep the product link and order record with any QC images, return request or shipping note. That creates a clearer chain from original listing to the actual parcel.
