How to Choose a PikoBuy Shipping Route

There is no universal best shipping route. The useful comparison is the one based on your actual parcel and destination.

Compare real parcel inputs

Use the correct delivery country, product type, weight and external parcel dimensions. A result prepared for a different country or a different category may not apply to your parcel. Recheck this information after warehouse review and any packing request.

Read billing information before payment

Route options can differ in billing method and delivery reference. Treat displayed information as current planning data, not as a guaranteed final charge or delivery date. Keep a record of the route, parcel inputs and any notices shown at submission.

Plan for cross-border uncertainty

International delivery can involve third-party logistics providers, customs activity and conditions outside a shopper’s control. Do not make a route choice based on a promise of clearance, a fixed arrival date or zero risk.
