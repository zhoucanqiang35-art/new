PikoBuy Research FAQ

Short, practical answers for shoppers who want to keep product research, warehouse checks and parcel planning clear.

Does PikoBuySheet process orders or payments?

No. This is an independent research guide. It does not process orders, payments, warehouse services or international deliveries.

Does a product link guarantee availability or suitability?

No. A link is a research record. Check the current product page, chosen variation, seller information and applicable conditions before ordering.

What can QC photos help confirm?

They can support visible checks of product type, variation, labels, quantity and obvious damage. They cannot prove authenticity, durability, internal performance or a customs result.

Why can a shipping estimate change?

Actual destination, product type, parcel weight, external dimensions, packaging and current route information can all affect a result.

Is a return guaranteed?

No. Eligibility may depend on seller rules, item condition, timing, category and the current process. A request is not the same as approval.
