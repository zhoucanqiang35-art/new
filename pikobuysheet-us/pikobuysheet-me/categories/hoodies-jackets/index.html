PikoBuy Hoodies & Jackets Research

Prioritise garment measurements, material details and the exact selected variation before making a parcel decision.

Use measurements, not a generic size promise

Different sellers may use different measurement methods and size references. Keep the original size chart with the product link and compare it with an item you already own when possible.

At warehouse check-in, verify visible labels, colour and quantity. Photos can show a visible mismatch but cannot prove fabric feel, fit or long-term wear.

Parcel planning

Bulky outerwear can affect parcel weight and dimensions. Recheck current parcel information before selecting an international route.
