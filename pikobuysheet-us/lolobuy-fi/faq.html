FAQ · source checked 7 September 2026

Clear answers about the workflow.

These answers reflect information shown on LoloBuy’s public homepage. They are not a guarantee about an individual order, price, route or delivery date.

What does LoloBuy say it does?

LoloBuy describes a service that assists customers with purchasing goods from China, receiving them at a China warehouse, inspecting them and submitting parcels for international delivery.

Can I start with a product link?

Yes. Its homepage provides a field for a product link or product name, and shows Chinese marketplace sources including Taobao, Weidian and 1688. Always confirm the selected variant before payment.

Does a marketplace price include every cost?

Not necessarily. Product price, China-side shipping, service charges and international freight can be separate. Read the current order and parcel totals shown for your own purchase.

What happens after a seller ships the item?

The platform describes a warehouse inspection and storage stage. This is the point to compare received-item photos with the original listing and resolve any important issue before parcel submission.

Are quality-inspection photos available?

LoloBuy’s public homepage states that it provides free quality-inspection pictures. Photos can help check visible details, but they cannot prove every measurement, material property or long-term durability.

Is storage free?

The homepage currently states “180 days free of storage charge.” Check the live policy for your account and order because service terms can change.

Can orders from different sellers be combined?

LoloBuy describes warehouse consolidation: purchases from different sellers can be integrated and submitted as one parcel. Check every item first, because an unresolved item can delay the group.

Are all shipping routes available for every parcel?

No assumption should be made. LoloBuy displays a broad route network, but availability can depend on destination, parcel size, contents and current carrier rules. Use the routes shown for your actual parcel.

Can this site quote customs, tax or delivery time?

No. Customs and tax obligations depend on the destination jurisdiction. Delivery estimates can change with carrier and customs conditions. Use official destination guidance for legal or tax decisions.

Is this an official LoloBuy website?

No. This is an independent research preview. It does not take payments, collect shipping details or process orders. Its only database destination is FindSpreadsheet.
