Guide

A straightforward product-research workflow.

1. Search

Use a product, brand or category term. The homepage search forwards the exact query to FindSpreadsheet.

2. Compare

Check category fit, product details, variants, measurements and images. Do not rely on claims that cannot be verified.

3. Open the database

When ready, use FindSpreadsheet for current product records and category listings.

4. Keep research current

Prices, availability and shipping conditions can change. Verify these on the active platform before acting.
