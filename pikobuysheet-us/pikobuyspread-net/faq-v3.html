FAQ

Clear answers before database browsing.

These answers reflect PikoBuy’s published workflow and distinguish public statements from details that need checking on a live order.

What does PikoBuy publicly say it does?

PikoBuy presents itself as a China shopping and forwarding service. Its public workflow describes product discovery, buy-for-me ordering, warehouse quality inspection and repacking, then a customer-selected shipping option.

Can a shopper search, submit a link or send an image?

Yes. PikoBuy’s public site says users can search for products directly or submit a product link, image or sourcing request. A request is a starting point for product confirmation, not proof that a specific item is available.

What does “buy for me” mean in the published workflow?

PikoBuy says it can purchase items from sellers according to a customer’s needs and centrally manage orders from different sources. It does not mean that every listing, seller description or product outcome is guaranteed.

What happens when an item reaches the China warehouse?

PikoBuy says items receive quality inspection and repacking upon warehouse arrival. That is a useful review stage, but visible checks and photos cannot guarantee fit, durability, authenticity or final delivery condition.

How should I use QC information?

Compare it against the exact listing, variant and checklist you saved earlier. Mark details as visible, stated by the seller or still unknown. Check category-specific points such as measurements, materials, parts, model numbers and pack contents.

Does repacking guarantee a lower shipping cost?

No. Repacking is part of the published warehouse workflow. The final shipping decision can still depend on parcel weight, dimensions, contents, route rules and destination requirements.

Does PikoBuy publish a fixed delivery time or shipping price for every parcel?

No universal figure should be assumed. PikoBuy provides a shipping-estimate entry point, but a current estimate depends on the actual parcel and destination. Treat old estimates and other buyers’ parcels as context, not a promise.

Can this independent site place an order or arrange shipping?

No. This site is for research and discovery only. It does not process payments, place orders or arrange shipping.

Where does the homepage search go?

The search field sends the exact keyword to FindSpreadsheet’s search page so you can open matching product content directly.

Where do the category buttons go?

Each right-side action opens the matching FindSpreadsheet category or keyword result. Watches and Socks use their dedicated FindSpreadsheet keyword result pages.

Does this site link to unrelated websites?

No. Its external actions point only to FindSpreadsheet.
