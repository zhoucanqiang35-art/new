FACT-BASED FAQ

Answers before
you place an order.

Short, specific answers based on PikoBuy’s public service pages. This independent guide does not operate the platform or make shipping promises.

What type of service does PikoBuy describe?

PikoBuy presents itself as a China purchasing and forwarding service. Its public homepage says users can search for products directly or submit product links, images or sourcing requests; it then describes purchase management, warehouse inspection and international shipping from its China warehouse.

Can I use a product link or do I need to search inside the platform?

The public beginner guide says users can paste a product link or keywords into the PikoBuy search box. The homepage also says product links, images and sourcing requests can be submitted. Before any request, confirm the exact specifications, colour, size and quantity from the original seller listing.

What happens if the seller has no stock?

The beginner guide says that if an item is out of stock, the order will be refunded. That is why it is sensible to keep one or two comparable finds rather than relying on a single spreadsheet row or product link.

What does warehouse inspection include?

PikoBuy’s guide describes warehouse check-in, photo confirmation and a defect check after items arrive. Its forwarding terms also say received goods are unpacked and inspected. These checks are useful for comparing the delivered item with the requested variant before international shipping.

Does inspection guarantee every technical or specialist detail?

No such guarantee is stated. The forwarding terms say professional inspection cannot be provided for special and professional products. For additional detail, the terms tell users to view inspection photos or purchase additional detailed photos. Ask focused questions about the evidence you need rather than assuming a specialist test has been performed.

When do I pay international shipping?

The beginner guide separates the purchase order from the parcel shipment. After the items reach the warehouse, the user chooses a shipping route, submits the parcel and pays the international shipping fee. This means the item decision and the international-shipping decision happen at different stages.

How should I use the shipping estimate?

The shipping-estimate page asks for destination, product type, weight and parcel dimensions. Use it for a preliminary comparison, then expect the final parcel characteristics to matter. Route billing methods and delivery times differ, according to the beginner guide, so an estimate should not be treated as a delivery guarantee.

When is tracking expected to appear?

PikoBuy’s beginner guide says tracking information will be available within three days after the parcel is shipped. This refers to the availability of tracking information; it is not the same as a guaranteed delivery date.

Who carries logistics and customs risk?

The shipping terms say parcels are carried by third-party logistics providers and that cross-border risks — including customs policy issues, damage, loss and peak-season delays — are risks users must assess and bear. The page says PikoBuy offers risk alerts and works on logistics insurance, but it does not promise that these risks disappear.

Can I return an item after it reaches the warehouse?

The public returns page describes a five-day return window after an item enters the warehouse, subject to the seller offering a return guarantee and the item meeting the seller’s resalable-condition rules. Some goods are not returnable, including examples such as intimate apparel, certain customised items and goods with removed packaging or labels. For no-fault returns, published fees can include return shipping, the seller’s original shipping and a service fee.

Is this site the official PikoBuy platform?

No. This is an independent research guide and discovery route. It does not sell products, receive payments, hold parcels, provide account support or make decisions about warehouse inspection, shipping or returns.
