LITBUY FAQ · FACT CHECKED

answers.

These answers summarise publicly listed LitBuy services. Fees, eligibility, route availability and after-sales outcomes can change, so confirm the live policy and order status before paying or submitting a parcel.

What marketplaces does LitBuy publicly list?

LitBuy’s public home page lists one-stop purchasing from 1688, Taobao and WeChat Store, along with DIY orders. A product still needs a clear link, chosen option and seller availability before it can be purchased.

Does LitBuy offer QC photos?

LitBuy publicly advertises free QC and inspection when an item reaches its warehouse, including inspection photos. Use those photos to compare the received item with the option you ordered; they are helpful evidence, not a guarantee of fit, authenticity or long-term durability.

How long can orders stay in storage?

LitBuy’s current public home page says storage is available for up to 120 days and that the first 90 days are free. Its Help Center also has a Storage Period page and notes that fees can apply after an overdue period. Check each order’s live storage counter.

Can several orders be shipped together?

LitBuy says orders can be shipped together and describes its forwarding service as sending items to the LitBuy warehouse for combination and international shipping. Consolidation can be useful, but it can also change packing size, route options and total shipping cost.

Is a Freight Estimator the final shipping price?

No. The estimator is a planning tool. Final international cost can depend on the packed parcel, destination, shipping line, weight or volume and services selected. Review the live parcel quote before payment.

How do I track an international parcel?

LitBuy provides a Track Shipment feature for international parcels. Keep the order ID, parcel details and tracking number. A scan is a status record, not a delivery guarantee, so allow for carrier handoffs and possible customs processing.

What after-sales help does LitBuy publicly list?

The Help Center lists domestic return instructions, after-sales requests for issues such as non-delivery, missing or damaged items, international parcel inspection rules, and insurance and compensation information. Read the live policy for the order and issue involved before assuming a return, refund or compensation result.

Can every item be shipped internationally?

No. LitBuy maintains prohibited-items and customs-information areas. Route eligibility also varies by destination. Check the product, route rules and applicable local law before ordering, especially for batteries, liquids, branded goods or other restricted categories.

What does packing and reinforcement mean?

LitBuy says it offers packing and reinforcement selected with item size in mind. More protection may be appropriate for fragile or shape-sensitive goods, but it can affect parcel dimensions. Choose it based on the item and the trade-off you accept.

Where can I learn the full workflow?

Read the LitBuy warehouse, QC and shipping guide for a step-by-step process: confirm the item, review QC, watch storage dates, compare live shipping options, and retain tracking records.
