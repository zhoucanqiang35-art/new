LITBUY GUIDE

Make every decision with the right evidence.

Buying from a Chinese marketplace is a chain of decisions: choose an exact product option, inspect the item at the warehouse, decide what to combine, then compare live parcel choices. Treat these as separate steps instead of assuming the merchandise price is the final delivered cost.

Start with the exact item record.

Retain the product link, option, size, colour and quantity. Seller availability can change, and a clear order record is the reference you need when the item reaches the warehouse.

Use QC photos as evidence.

LitBuy publicly describes QC and inspection photos. Compare the received item with the selected option. Photos help verify visible details, but cannot guarantee fit, hidden defects, authenticity or long-term durability.

Compare the parcel after packing data exists.

Storage and consolidation can be useful, but packing choices, volumetric weight, available routes and services can change a live quote. A Freight Estimator is a planning tool; review the final parcel quote before payment.

Keep tracking records together.

Save the order ID, QC photos, parcel record and tracking number. Tracking is an event record rather than a delivery guarantee: carrier handoffs and customs processing can affect scans and delivery times.
