LITBUY GUIDE

Make every decision with the right evidence.

Buying from a Chinese marketplace is a chain of decisions: choose an exact product option, inspect the item at the warehouse, decide what to combine, then compare live parcel choices. Treat those as separate steps instead of assuming the merchandise price is the final delivered cost.

LitBuy publicly lists purchasing from 1688, Taobao and WeChat Store, together with DIY orders. Its public tools include warehouse storage, QC and inspection photos, consolidation, a Freight Estimator, tracking and after-sales policy pages.

When QC photos arrive, compare the received item with the source link, chosen option, size and quantity. A photo is evidence for visible details and option accuracy; it cannot prove fit, hidden defects, authenticity or long-term durability.

Use storage deliberately. A combined parcel may change packing needs, volumetric weight and available routes. Compare the final live quote after parcel data is available, and keep the order ID, QC photos, parcel record and tracking number together.

The fact-based FAQ is presented below as its own homepage module; the separate FAQ page remains available too.

What marketplaces does LitBuy publicly list?

LitBuy’s public home page lists one-stop purchasing from 1688, Taobao and WeChat Store, along with DIY orders.

Does LitBuy offer QC photos?

LitBuy publicly advertises free QC and inspection when an item reaches its warehouse, including inspection photos.

How long can orders stay in storage?

Check each order’s live storage counter and the applicable Storage Period policy before planning a consolidation.

Is a Freight Estimator the final shipping price?

No. Final cost depends on the packed parcel, destination, shipping line, weight or volume and services selected.

How do I track an international parcel?

Keep the order ID, parcel details and tracking number. A scan is a status record, not a delivery guarantee.
