# UK shipping research

Checked 2026-11-05. The short guide is general planning advice and makes no fixed rate or transit-time claims. UK government guidance states that VAT, duty, and courier charges may apply depending on goods, destination, and circumstances; different geographic rules can apply to Northern Ireland. The buyer should check current official guidance and the actual transaction.
