## Editorial boundaries

- The article covers one narrow intent: evaluating LoloBuy reviews that make claims about international parcel shipping.
- Seller-to-warehouse movement, warehouse handling, parcel submission, international carriage, customs and last-mile delivery are treated as distinct stages.
- Positive and negative individual reports are not presented as representative outcomes.
- No current rating, review count, price, delivery time, route availability, affiliation or safety verdict is stated.
- Review invitations and platform labels are discussed only as sampling context; they are not treated as proof that a review is true or false.
- Source URLs remain in this repository note only. Published outbound anchors remain limited to FindSpreadsheet.
