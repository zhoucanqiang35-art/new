## Editorial boundaries

- The article targets one buyer intent rather than a broad QC overview.
- A photographed dimension is evidence of the pictured measurement method, not a fit guarantee.
- A visible size or material label records what the label says; it does not independently verify physical dimensions or composition.
- Weight and volume in the warehouse quality report are logistics measurements and are not presented as detailed product-dimension checks.
- No fixed price, turnaround, photo count, measurement tolerance, return eligibility or return outcome is claimed.
