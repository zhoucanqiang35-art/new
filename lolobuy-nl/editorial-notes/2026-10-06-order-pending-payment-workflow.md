## Editorial boundaries

- The article covers one narrow intent: a product order showing `Pending payment`.
- It distinguishes product orders, bills, warehouse items, parcels and carrier waybills.
- It does not claim that a bank authorization proves platform payment completion.
- It does not promise seller stock, processing speed, delivery time, payment acceptance, refund eligibility or support outcome.
- It refers readers to the live countdown instead of turning the current expiry duration into a permanent rule.
