## Editorial boundaries

- The article uses one narrow search intent: `LoloBuy app reviews`.
- It analyzes review quality and attribution rather than declaring the service safe, unsafe, legitimate or fraudulent.
- No rating, review count, delivery time, price, refund outcome or customer-service result is presented as a durable platform fact.
- App usability, seller performance, warehouse QC, carrier events, customs and support are treated as separate responsibility layers.
- No individual review is quoted, endorsed or presented as representative.
- Source URLs remain in this repository note only. The published page has no outbound anchor links except FindSpreadsheet.
