HipoBuy QC photos: a practical checklist

This is an independent research and discovery website. It is not an official Hipobuy website and does not sell or process products.

Find

Match each photograph to the exact listing and chosen colour, size and quantity. Check overall shape, front and back, seams, marks, fasteners and any visible damage. Lighting and camera angles can affect colour and proportions.

Check

For clothing, compare the measured chest, length and shoulders with a garment that fits you; for shoes, check the size label and, when available, the insole measurement. Request a clearer view if an important detail is missing.

Decide

QC images document visible features of an item at a particular moment. They cannot establish authenticity, material composition, electrical safety or long-term performance. Check current return or exchange terms before approving shipment.
