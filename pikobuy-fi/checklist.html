Before you continue

Product review checklist.

Use these checks before opening a final purchase route. They keep product discovery, QC review and parcel decisions separate.

01

Check the source

Confirm the live listing still describes the same item and selected option.

02

Review useful images

Look for material, shape, colour and key angles rather than one marketing image.

03

Use size context

Read measurements when fit matters; do not rely only on a letter size.

04

Inspect QC evidence

Use warehouse photos as a practical check while the item remains actionable.

05

Plan the parcel

Keep destination, dimensions and route decisions separate from product hype.

06

Open the official route

Continue to PikoBuy only after the product and evidence still fit your plan.
