FAQ · practical answers

Questions worth answering before a parcel moves.

These quick answers mirror the full independent FAQ without replacing it. They help separate source research, QC review and parcel decisions.

Product research

Is this a checkout?

No. This site is an independent product guide; registration, payment and final purchasing happen on PikoBuy.

What should I check in a product link?

Confirm the exact size, colour, version, source photos and option before paying. A title or price snapshot alone can change.

Warehouse QC

What can QC photos confirm?

They can show visible colour, shape, packaging, accessories and obvious marks. They do not guarantee authenticity, materials or durability.

When should I review a return?

Review promptly after warehouse arrival. Eligible returns may have a five-day, 120-hour window, subject to live seller and platform rules.

Parcel planning

Why can a shipping estimate change?

Destination, packed weight, dimensions and route billing can change the final result. Use estimates as planning context, not a promise.

When does tracking appear?

PikoBuy says tracking is normally available within three days after shipment; carrier scans and handoffs can take longer.

Can customs risk be removed?

No. Check live route and prohibited-item rules before submitting a parcel; international shipping involves third-party carriers and destination rules.
