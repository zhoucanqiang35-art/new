How to evaluate a product listing before you add it to a haul

1. Check the source

Confirm the original product URL, the selected variant, the available measurements and the current price before you compare alternatives.

2. Use QC as evidence

Quality-control photos can show the actual stored item, but they are not a universal quality guarantee. Review the details that matter for your own order.

3. Plan the parcel

Weight, dimensions, destination rules and shipping method influence the final decision. Keep these variables separate from the item price.

4. Browse responsibly

When you are ready to continue, use the category route to research available items on FindsSpreadsheet.
