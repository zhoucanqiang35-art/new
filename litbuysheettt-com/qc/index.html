QC GUIDE

Research visible details before you use a live route.

A QC-style review helps you compare the information you can see. It cannot establish authenticity, final quality, inventory, delivery time, or any seller promise.

01

Match the exact option

Keep the product link, selected variant, size field, colour and quantity together. A broad product name is not enough to identify the right item.

02

Compare more than one image

Review construction, material texture, colour and proportions across available angles. Note what is missing instead of filling gaps with assumptions.

03

Check live details again

Before moving on, read the current listing, active price, measurements and route restrictions. These may change after an earlier screenshot or saved link.

04

Keep a decision record

Save the variant, size, price context and relevant product notes so you can compare them with the active order information later.

05

Use evidence, not promises

Images and listing copy can inform a question; they are not a guarantee about materials, authenticity, stock or after-sales results.

06

Choose the focused category

Use the matching category route rather than browsing an unrelated list. It makes product research faster and easier to verify.

How to review LitBuy QC photos before shipping

A warehouse photo shows the item that arrived, while the seller’s listing describes what was offered. Start by putting the order record, selected option and available photos side by side. Check the item name, quantity, colour and size label against your saved order. If the photos do not show a label or an important detail, record that gap instead of assuming it is correct.

Check shoes, clothing and accessories differently

For shoes, compare both sides, the toe, heel and sole; look for visible damage, mismatched pairs and the size marking. For a hoodie or jacket, inspect front and back, seams, zips, prints and fabric appearance. Compare measured width and length with a garment you already own, using the seller’s measurement method. For bags and accessories, check closures, straps, hardware and included pieces. Photos cannot reliably establish durability, comfort, material composition or authenticity.

When a photo raises a question

Contact the service through the relevant order while the item is still in the warehouse. Describe the specific uncertainty and request a useful angle or measurement if available; check the current price and conditions for any extra service. Review the seller’s and service’s current return or exchange terms before making a decision. A photo from another buyer or listing is a reference, not evidence of the item in your own order.

Before you choose a shipping route

Review the current parcel details, packing options, destination restrictions and total shown at checkout. Shipping estimates can change with packed dimensions, weight and route; taxes or customs charges may also apply. Keep order and tracking information private.
