LitBuy FAQ: What the Public Order and Forwarding Flows Actually Show

These answers summarize LitBuy’s publicly visible Fill&Buy and Forwarding Orders screens. They are for research only. Current order details, fees, policy terms, and staff confirmation should always decide an individual transaction.

Is this an official LitBuy support page?

No. LitBuy Sheet is an independent research guide. It does not process orders, collect payments, arrange shipment, or make decisions for LitBuy. Its product and database actions route to FindSpreadsheet.

What is the difference between Fill&Buy and Forwarding Orders?

LitBuy publicly presents Fill&Buy as a purchase-assistance form for submitting product details. Forwarding Orders is for registering a parcel being sent to the LitBuy warehouse. Both flows later involve warehouse handling and international-shipping payment, but they start from different situations.

What does the Fill&Buy form ask a buyer to provide?

The public page asks for a product link, product name, notes, unit price, quantity, and domestic-shipping information. It also allows supporting product images. Complete, precise item details are more useful than a broad title or a screenshot alone.

Can LitBuy verify product quality or authenticity?

LitBuy’s publicly displayed disclaimer says it cannot verify product quality or authenticity and asks users to make their own judgment. Do not turn product photos, an agent workflow, or an independent guide into a guarantee about an item.

What are the public steps for a Fill&Buy order?

The page shows five stages: submit order; storage and inspection; submit parcel; pay international shipping; and wait for delivery. The actual order status and order-page information remain the source of truth for a live request.

What information is needed for forwarding?

LitBuy’s public forwarding screen asks for a tracking number, parcel name, and declared parcel value. It describes the tracking number as the forwarding order’s only identification and advises users not to disclose it to others.

When can a forwarded parcel be packed and shipped?

The public instructions say that after a tracking number is submitted, an order ID is generated. Once the order status shows “Stored,” the user can go to Warehouse, select items for packing and shipping, enter a delivery address, and pay the related fees.

Is international shipping included in the initial purchase amount?

No universal total should be assumed. The Fill&Buy page says international shipping is charged separately. The live parcel, route, address, and current fee display should be checked before payment.

Can I rely on a published delivery time or shipping price?

No. An independent guide should not promise a fixed rate or delivery date. Route availability, parcel details, destination requirements, and current terms can change. Check the live order and shipping information at the point of action.

How many product images can be attached to a Fill&Buy request?

The public Fill&Buy screen states that up to five images can be uploaded, with each file under 2 MB in GIF, JPG, or PNG format. Use images to clarify an item, but keep the item link, selected variant, and notes accurate as well.

What does the public return warning say?

LitBuy’s displayed third-party-source disclaimer states that returns without quality issues or merchant-related problems are not accepted for the products described there. This does not predict any individual result; read the current live refund and return terms before ordering.

What should I check before paying for shipping?

Verify the current order status, selected items, delivery address, parcel information, live charges, route restrictions, and prohibited-items guidance. Preserve your original item details so you can compare them against the active order before you commit.
