How to use PikoBuy: from listing to parcel

Choose a product and confirm the seller listing, variation, size and quantity. PikoBuy describes an initial item payment, followed by warehouse inspection and a separate international shipping payment.

Review the warehouse photos before submitting the parcel. Check the current route, fees and return terms, then keep the tracking details after dispatch.

What PikoBuy publicly describes

PikoBuy presents a six-stage flow. First, users select an item on marketplaces such as Taobao, Tmall, 1688, Weidian or Yupoo and confirm the specification, colour and size. Next, they can paste a product link or keywords into PikoBuy. The public beginner guide then describes submitting an order and an initial payment, warehouse inspection after arrival, choosing and paying for an international shipping route, and waiting for tracking after dispatch.

That description is useful because it shows where the decision changes. A product link is not the final parcel. The final parcel is created only after the correct variation is purchased, the warehouse receives it, the item is inspected, packaging choices are considered and a shipping route is selected. Treat every stage as a chance to catch a mismatch before the next cost is committed.

Start with a record you can verify

Before submitting a link, save the live listing URL, the exact variation, size, colour, quantity, listed price and the photos that explain why the item is on your shortlist. PikoBuy says it can help confirm product details and proceed with purchasing, but its terms also say it is not normally the manufacturer or seller of record. That means a buyer should not turn a broad title or one promotional image into a claim about materials, fit, authenticity, performance or seller reliability.

A simple record makes later QC more useful. For clothing, save the size chart and material description. For shoes, save side, sole, heel and size information. For jewellery, record dimensions, fastening style and visible finish. For electronics, record the exact model, plug or voltage requirement, included accessories and compatibility claim. If a listing does not state the detail that matters, write down the uncertainty instead of filling it with an assumption.

Returns are conditional and time-sensitive

The returns page calls its service a 5-Day Return Guarantee. Its stated rule is that a return request must be made within five days after an order becomes In Warehouse, described there as 120 hours. Eligibility also depends on the seller offering a return guarantee and the item remaining in the resalable condition the seller requires. The platform lists categories that may not be returnable, including customized goods, second-hand goods, intimate apparel, food and other seller-specified exclusions.

Do not assume that a warehouse arrival automatically creates a free cancellation. The page states that a no-fault return can include shipping back to the seller, the seller's original shipping fee and an RMB 5 service fee. It also says seller consent is required for a return. The right time to read those details is before ordering and again immediately after warehouse status changes, not after the return window has expired.
