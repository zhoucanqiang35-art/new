PikoBuy QC photos: a practical checklist

Compare the warehouse photos with the exact listing, size, colour and quantity. Check seams, measurements, accessories and any visible defects. If an important detail is missing, request clarification before making a shipping decision.

Photos help identify visible differences, but cannot prove authenticity, durability or fit. Save the listing and photos so that you can discuss any discrepancy with support.

Start with a record you can verify

Before submitting a link, save the live listing URL, the exact variation, size, colour, quantity, listed price and the photos that explain why the item is on your shortlist. PikoBuy says it can help confirm product details and proceed with purchasing, but its terms also say it is not normally the manufacturer or seller of record. That means a buyer should not turn a broad title or one promotional image into a claim about materials, fit, authenticity, performance or seller reliability.

A simple record makes later QC more useful. For clothing, save the size chart and material description. For shoes, save side, sole, heel and size information. For jewellery, record dimensions, fastening style and visible finish. For electronics, record the exact model, plug or voltage requirement, included accessories and compatibility claim. If a listing does not state the detail that matters, write down the uncertainty instead of filling it with an assumption.

Use warehouse QC as evidence, not a guarantee

PikoBuy says items are inspected when they arrive at its China warehouse. Its beginner guide names check-in, photo confirmation and defect checking; its shipping terms say forwarded goods are unpacked and inspected and that buyers can view inspection photos or purchase additional detailed photos. Those are concrete service statements. They are not a statement that every hidden defect, fit issue, material claim or future performance problem can be discovered from warehouse photos.

The most reliable QC habit is comparison. Put the warehouse photos next to the saved listing record and ask: is this the same colour and variation? Is the label, measurement, component, logo placement, plug, clasp, sole or screen detail visible enough for the risk of this category? Has the seller included every listed accessory? Does the item still make sense at the likely parcel weight? When a key point is not visible, treat it as unresolved. A clear photo can support a decision; it cannot replace the live listing or create a warranty.

PikoBuy's guide also mentions requests such as minimal or reinforced packaging. Use such requests as handling preferences, not as a universal promise. They may change protection, package size or the information available for a later claim. The right choice depends on the item and on the current route.
