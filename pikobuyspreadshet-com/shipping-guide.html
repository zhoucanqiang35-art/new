Shipping research

The listed item price is not the parcel decision.

The official PikoBuy estimator requests destination country or region, product type, weight and dimensions. That tells us what users must research before selecting a route.

Research questionWhy it matters
What is the actual item and package weight?Heavy outerwear, shoes, bags and electronics can change value after packing.
What are the package dimensions?Some routes bill using dimensional weight rather than item weight alone.
Which destination country or region?Available options and costs can vary by destination and current route rules.
What is the final official quote?Estimates are planning input, not a promise of the paid total or delivery date.

Use facts, not invented shipping times

PikoBuy states that routes differ in delivery time and billing method. Its shipping terms also say third-party logistics involve customs and other cross-border risks. This guide therefore does not publish fixed delivery-time promises or customs outcomes.
