Practical method

Start with the exact product option, record the item ID, selected variant, visible materials, quantity and current images.

Use the current listing and warehouse information as evidence. Older screenshots and copied names are leads, not a current confirmation.

Keep the question specific: what is the item, where will it go, and what needs to be checked?

When information is uncertain, stop at that uncertainty; an image or route label does not guarantee quality, carrier acceptance, customs clearance or delivery.
