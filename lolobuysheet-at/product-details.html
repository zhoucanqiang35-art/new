01

Design point of view

Midnight Layer is designed as a quiet statement piece: clean at first glance, then more considered in its long line, softened shoulder and restrained colour story. The reference is about balance rather than decoration.

02

Silhouette and fit

The intended shape sits away from the body without becoming shapeless. A dropped shoulder and straight fall make room for a T-shirt or fine knit underneath, while the length keeps the look deliberate rather than oversized for its own sake.

03

Texture and finish

This is a style brief, not a material guarantee. Look for a surface that appears matte and substantial, with minimal shine and a finish that lets seams, cuffs and neckline keep their definition in everyday wear.

04

Colour direction

The palette starts with deep espresso and moves through charcoal, soft rose and muted blue-grey as accent options. Keep the base dark and let one lighter tone do the work; that makes the piece easier to repeat with denim, tailored trousers or relaxed shorts.

05

How to use this reference
