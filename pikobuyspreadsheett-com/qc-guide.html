QC evidence

QC photos are evidence—not an automatic approval.

PikoBuy’s official guide says warehouse inspection includes check-in, photo confirmation and defect checking. Your task is to compare those photos with the listing and the item category.

Use the same seven checks for every shortlist

  • Correct category and comparable items
  • Photos show the details that matter
  • Visible measurements or fit notes
  • Source link matches the item
  • Price has real context
  • Expected weight remains reasonable
  • You can explain why the item stays

What changes by category?

Shoes need side, sole and heel photos. Apparel needs measurements and fabric detail. Bags, watches and accessories need dimensions, hardware and close-ups. A title cannot replace missing evidence.
