FAQ

Questions shoppers should answer first

What does the public LoloBuy homepage say about payment?

Its visible process panel says shoppers can browse product orders and pay product prices and freight in mainland China. Confirm the exact order amount, currency and status in the live platform before payment.

Does the homepage mention warehouse inspection?

Yes. The public panel mentions warehousing quality inspection and quality-inspection pictures. Photos can help compare an item with the listing, but they do not guarantee fit, durability, authenticity or delivery.

Is “180 days free storage” a guaranteed term for every order?

It is displayed on the public homepage panel we reviewed. Check the current storage terms shown for your order before relying on it, including start date, eligibility and any conditions that may apply.

What does “100 logistics routes” prove?

The public panel uses that phrase when describing logistics choices. It does not prove that a particular route is available for your destination, suitable for your goods, fixed in price or guaranteed to arrive by a date.

Can combined delivery be assumed for every item?

The homepage refers to selecting goods for combined delivery. Confirm eligibility, parcel restrictions, the current quote and destination-specific options inside the platform for the actual items you hold.

How should I use quality-inspection pictures?

Compare the pictures with the product link, selected variant, quantity and the details you wrote down before payment. Ask for a specific clarification if an important point is not visible.

Does this research guide process payments or place orders?

No. This is an independent research guide. It does not process payments, place orders, provide customer support or promise shipping, customs or product outcomes.

Where should live product and category research happen?

Use the database links on this site to compare current product information on FindsSpreadsheet. Verify all final platform rules, prices, availability and shipping choices in the relevant order flow.
