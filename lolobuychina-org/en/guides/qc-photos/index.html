LoloBuy QC Photos: What to Check Before You Ship

A practical QC-photo checklist for comparing a LoloBuy warehouse item with your product link and selected option.

Start with identity, not appearance,Open the original listing and your order record before you inspect a warehouse photo. Confirm the item type, selected colour or variant, size, quantity and any included pieces. A tidy-looking photo cannot correct the wrong option. Keep the listing link and a short written record together so your decision is based on the order you actually placed.,Check visible condition with focused questions,Look for obvious damage, stains, missing pieces, uneven shape or a visible mismatch. Then ask one question at a time: can the label be read, is a measurement visible, is the left side shown, are both pieces included? Photos document what was photographed; they do not prove fit, durability, authenticity or future availability.,Use measurements where a size matters,Do not infer fit from an S, M or L label alone. Compare a visible measurement with the seller chart and with an item you own. If the evidence does not answer the question that mattered before payment, keep it open. A specific clarification is more useful than a broad request to check whether an item is good.,Decide before parcel submission,QC is most useful before an international parcel is submitted. Approve only when the images answer your non-negotiable questions. If they do not, pause and use the relevant live platform options to clarify the point. Keep screenshots or notes of the order, visible evidence and decision; this separates a documented choice from a hopeful assumption.,A short QC checklist,Match link, variant, size, quantity and included pieces. Check visible condition and any category-specific detail. Compare measurements where possible. Record what is unclear, then resolve it before selecting international shipping.
