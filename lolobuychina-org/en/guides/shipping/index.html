LoloBuy Shipping Guide: Combined Parcels, Weight and Route Checks

How to compare a LoloBuy parcel quote using actual details instead of a headline shipping estimate.

Wait for parcel facts,A shipping estimate becomes useful only when it reflects the parcel you actually hold. Confirm destination, item type, measured weight, dimensions and packaging choice in the current order flow. A quote from another destination, another service or an earlier screen is not a promise for your parcel.,Compare actual and volumetric weight,Some services can charge by actual weight or by dimensional weight. A light but bulky parcel can therefore be billed differently from a compact one. Do not assume a per-kilogram figure tells the whole story. Compare services using the same parcel contents and the displayed rules for the current destination.,Use consolidation deliberately,Combined shipping can reduce repeated handling, but it also changes parcel size, weight and eligibility. Keep a simple list of items that have arrived, items that still need QC and items ready to ship. Only group goods after checking that their contents, route restrictions and displayed parcel details work together.,Read the live route conditions,A route name, a general delivery estimate or a public statement about logistics choices does not guarantee acceptance, clearance or a delivery date. Review the live quote, restrictions, declared-information requirements and service conditions for your destination. Use accurate information and follow applicable customs and carrier rules.,A short shipping checklist,Confirm contents, destination, measured parcel data and current quote. Compare like with like: the same parcel, service type and timing. Check restrictions before paying. Save the order status and quote that supported your decision.
