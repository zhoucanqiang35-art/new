How to Use a LoloBuy Product Link: Seller Listing and Order Checks

A step-by-step research guide to checking a product link, selected variant and seller details before using LoloBuy.

Treat the product link as a record,A product link is the starting evidence for an order, not a guarantee that the product will remain available. Save the link, title, selected option, displayed size notes, quantity and relevant seller comments before payment. This makes it possible to see whether a later order screen still matches the item you intended to buy.,Read variants instead of guessing,Different colours, sizes and bundles can have different images, stock notes or measurements. Open the selected option and check it against the image and written description. If the listing is unclear about a material, included piece or measurement, write the exact question before you move forward.,Separate the costs,Keep product price, domestic freight, international shipping, taxes and optional services as separate lines. A product price does not answer the international parcel cost, and an early estimate is not a final quote. Confirm currency, amount and order status in the live platform at each commitment.,Use warehouse evidence as the next check,After an item reaches a warehouse, compare available inspection photos with the original listing and saved option. This is the point to confirm visible identity and raise a targeted question. It is not an authenticity certificate or a substitute for reading the seller listing before payment.,A short product-link checklist,Save the exact link and option. Check images, title, size notes and quantity. Record the questions that could change your decision. Separate each cost. Compare warehouse evidence with the original record before sending a parcel.
