How to Order on HipoBuy: A First-Order Checklist

Independent product research

Start with the details

Start by identifying the exact product and variant you want. Compare the seller’s listing, size chart, product images and current price. A spreadsheet or product directory can help you find candidates, but it does not reserve stock or replace the live listing.

Before confirming an order, check the option, quantity, currency and current amount shown at checkout. Payment methods and terms can vary by account and destination. Save the order reference and record the link and variant so that you can compare them with later updates.

When evidence becomes available, review the item’s visible condition and any QC photos against your record. If a size or detail is important and cannot be seen, request clarification where available. Check the relevant seller and service rules before relying on a return or exchange.

Compare the evidence

Before submitting a product, verify that the listing is still active and that the option selected matches your notes. A seller can group several versions under one title, and the price displayed before selection may not apply to every variant. Check the size chart, colour, quantity and any domestic delivery charge on the current screen.

Keep a simple record of the product link, selected option, displayed amount and the time you checked it. If the listing changes, this record helps explain which version you intended to order. It does not reserve stock or guarantee that the seller will fulfil the order.

At payment, read the currency, final amount and terms shown for the available method. A payment logo on a public page does not promise that the same option is available for every destination or account. Retain your confirmation and avoid treating the item price as the cost of international delivery.

Make the decision

When the item reaches a stage where inspection evidence is available, compare the images with your recorded option. Check quantity, visible condition and the measurements that matter to you. If the evidence is insufficient, request clarification where the service permits it and review any applicable deadlines.

Make the parcel decision only after considering the items you intend to send together. Review weight, dimensions, route restrictions, destination and the final quote. Once dispatched, follow the carrier’s tracking and preserve the order and parcel references separately. Contact the responsible service when a specific stage needs clarification.

For international dispatch, review the actual items, packed measurements, available routes, costs and destination requirements in the current parcel flow. Keep tracking and payment records. This independent guide helps with research; it does not handle payment, fulfilment or customer support.
