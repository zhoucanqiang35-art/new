How to Order on HipoBuy: A First-Order Checklist

Independent product research

Start with the details

Start by identifying the exact product and variant you want. Compare the seller’s listing, size chart, product images and current price. A spreadsheet or product directory can help you find candidates, but it does not reserve stock or replace the live listing.

Before confirming an order, check the option, quantity, currency and current amount shown at checkout. Payment methods and terms can vary by account and destination. Save the order reference and record the link and variant so that you can compare them with later updates.

When evidence becomes available, review the item’s visible condition and any QC photos against your record. If a size or detail is important and cannot be seen, request clarification where available. Check the relevant seller and service rules before relying on a return or exchange.

Begin with a specific item rather than a broad collection of links. Record the live product page, seller, intended colour or size, quantity and the date you checked the listing. If an option label is unclear, compare the seller’s images and description or seek clarification. A spreadsheet helps organise candidates but does not reserve stock or guarantee that the same listing will remain available.

Compare the evidence

Check the size information before committing. For garments, compare the seller’s measurement points and units with a similar item that fits; for shoes, distinguish foot length from insole or external sole measurements. A size letter alone is not a complete fit description. Record any unanswered questions so you can revisit them if inspection measurements are available later.

Read the selected option and current item price together. Listings can group multiple colours, bundles or versions, so the initial price may not apply to your selection. Check the quantity and any domestic delivery charge shown in the active flow. Keep the order record and avoid assuming that a product price includes international shipping or destination charges.

At payment, confirm the amount, currency and terms displayed for the method actually available to your account and destination. Public payment logos are not a guarantee of availability or a statement of protection for every transaction. Save the confirmation and order reference. The independent research site does not take the payment or have access to your order.

Once the item enters the service’s order process, read each status in context. A seller’s dispatch, warehouse arrival and international parcel handoff are different events and may have different references. If a status is unclear, retain the date and contact the responsible service with the order reference rather than inferring a universal processing time.

Make the decision

When inspection photos or measurements are available, compare them with your selected option and notes. Check visible condition, colour, quantity, labels and the measurements that matter. Ask for a clear additional view where available if an important detail is hidden. Photos help identify visible differences but do not prove durability, authenticity or how an item will fit.

Before seeking a return or exchange, read the current seller and service terms, including eligibility, deadlines and any applicable charges. Preserve relevant packaging and document the issue. If the evidence is insufficient or the service must contact the seller, allow the stated process to determine the outcome; do not assume every item qualifies for a refund.

Plan international delivery as a separate decision. Compare the actual parcel contents, packed weight and dimensions, destination, route restrictions, optional services and final quote. Provide accurate information and account for possible destination-side charges. Keep the parcel tracking number separate from the item order reference, and use the carrier’s updates to understand the delivery stage.

For international dispatch, review the actual items, packed measurements, available routes, costs and destination requirements in the current parcel flow. Keep tracking and payment records. This independent guide helps with research; it does not handle payment, fulfilment or customer support.
