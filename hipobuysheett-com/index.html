How to research product options before shipping

Start with the exact item name, variant and size. Product photos are useful for comparing color and construction, while QC photographs and measurements provide the final decision points once an item reaches the warehouse.

For international orders, prepare the parcel only after reviewing item count, packed-weight estimate and destination options. Keep product links and size notes together so the selected variant can be checked before submission.
