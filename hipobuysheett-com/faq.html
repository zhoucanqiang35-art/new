FAQ: clear limits, useful checks

Basis: Hipobuy’s public website was reviewed on 24 September 2026. This independent guide does not claim to be Hipobuy support; always confirm the live rule shown in your account before you pay or ship.

Is this website Hipobuy?

No. It is an independent research layer. Its external product links go only to FindSpreadsheet; it does not accept orders, payments, parcels or support requests.

What does Hipobuy publicly present itself as?

The public homepage describes Hipobuy as a China shopping agent. In practical terms, a buyer can use a product link or record, arrange purchase through an agent workflow, then make a separate international parcel decision after the item reaches the warehouse.

Are warehouse or QC photos a quality guarantee?

No. Images can document visible details at one moment: colour, shape, label, obvious marks, accessories or a requested measurement. They cannot prove authenticity, comfort, durability, future performance, material composition or customs eligibility. Treat them as evidence, not a verdict.

What should I compare in photos?

Compare the exact option you ordered with the visible item: size tag, colour, logo or print placement, hardware, pair symmetry, outer packaging and included parts. For a decision-critical point, request a specific angle or measurement rather than “more QC.”

Can I rely on an old shipping price or line recommendation?

No. Routes, eligibility, packed dimensions, destination rules and surcharges can change. Use any online estimator or selection screen as a current quote, then re-check the final parcel shown in your account before paying.

Does consolidation make every parcel cheaper?

Not automatically. Combining items may reduce duplicated handling, but a larger box, volumetric weight, restricted contents or a different available line can change the result. Compare actual packed dimensions and eligible routes, not just item count.

How soon should I inspect a newly stored item?

As soon as the pictures and status are available. Return or exchange rules can be time-limited and seller-dependent. Do not confuse warehouse storage time with a right to return, and do not wait for the rest of a haul before checking an item with a visible problem.

Can a shopping agent promise customs clearance?

No responsible guide should promise that. Customs decisions are made by relevant authorities and depend on destination, declaration, parcel contents and current rules. Check destination guidance and do not treat a previous buyer’s outcome as a guarantee.

What should I save before a return or support request?

Keep the item link, chosen option, order note, warehouse photos, screenshots of the relevant status and any seller or support messages. Describe the discrepancy plainly and attach the image that shows it.

Do product cards show a guaranteed price?

No. Dollar figures marked “reference” are for orientation only. Listing price, domestic delivery, exchange rate, service charges and international shipping can change. Open the linked source record and confirm the current amount yourself.

Why does the language menu show eight options?

EN, DE, FR, ES, IT, PT, NL and PL are provided as a persistent reading preference. Selecting one keeps the selected language while navigating; it does not alter product availability, prices or platform rules.
