PikoBuy Forwarding Guide: Send China Orders to the Warehouse

Already buying from a Chinese seller yourself? Build a traceable handoff from the seller to PikoBuy’s warehouse, then make a separate international parcel decision.

Independent research · reviewed 15 September 2026

PikoBuy forwarding is for a different buying situation from its purchasing service. Instead of asking the platform to place the original order, you arrange the purchase with a seller and direct that seller or sender to PikoBuy’s warehouse in China. PikoBuy’s published forwarding terms then describe warehouse receipt, photos, storage and a later international parcel submission.

That distinction matters. A forwarding warehouse can receive and move an order, but it does not automatically inherit the seller conversation, payment record, product promises or after-sales responsibility from your original transaction. The safest approach is to treat forwarding as a documented chain of custody: identify the incoming shipment, preserve the seller evidence and make a fresh decision before paying for international delivery.

When forwarding makes sense

Forwarding can fit buyers who are able to order directly from a China-based seller, have an existing supplier relationship or need to combine independently purchased goods at one warehouse. PikoBuy’s terms tell forwarding users to obtain its warehouse address, give that address to the sender, complete a forwarding form and wait for warehouse receipt. This is not the same workflow as pasting a product link and asking PikoBuy to buy the item.

Choose the path before paying the seller. If you need PikoBuy to confirm a listing and place the purchase, begin with the PikoBuy product search guide. If you will pay the seller yourself, your forwarding record needs to connect the seller’s domestic parcel with your PikoBuy account. Mixing the two paths can leave everyone with an incomplete idea of who ordered, who paid and who must handle a dispute.

Get the warehouse address from the current official channel

PikoBuy’s shipping terms say to obtain the warehouse address through online customer service or the forwarding order form page. Use the address shown for your own account and shipment at the time you send it. Do not rely on an old screenshot, a copied address from another buyer or an unofficial post. Warehouses, recipient codes and formatting requirements can change.

Copy every field exactly, including the recipient name, telephone number, region, street details and any account identifier PikoBuy provides. Give the seller the complete address in a form its domestic carrier can use. Before dispatch, ask the seller to send back the formatted address or label for comparison. A correct street address without the required identifying reference may still make account matching harder.

Create an incoming-parcel record before the seller ships

The official process requires the forwarding form to be completed and submitted. Prepare the supporting information before the parcel moves: seller or sender name, marketplace order number, domestic tracking number, carrier, number of cartons, product description, quantity and the date dispatched. If one order is split into several domestic parcels, record each tracking number separately rather than assuming the warehouse will know they belong together.

Keep a seller invoice or order screenshot showing the exact variant and quantity. For a multi-item shipment, make a simple line-by-line list. Our China shopping spreadsheet guide shows how to preserve source, option and evidence fields. The goal is not paperwork for its own sake; it is a reliable comparison when the warehouse record appears.

Confirm domestic postage and avoid surprise COD charges

Clarify whether the seller is sending the parcel prepaid or cash on delivery. PikoBuy’s forwarding terms state that when an incoming parcel’s shipping fee is cash on delivery, the fee will be deducted from the buyer’s account balance without confirmation. The policy advises keeping enough balance available so the goods can be received smoothly.

Record the seller’s domestic freight promise and the tracking number before shipment. If you did not agree to COD, address that with the sender immediately. The domestic leg is a separate cost and evidence trail from the later international shipment. A product advertised with “free shipping” may refer only to a marketplace arrangement or one delivery segment; do not apply that phrase to the entire China-to-home journey.

Track the handoff to the warehouse

Watch the carrier’s domestic tracking, but treat “delivered” as the start of warehouse reconciliation rather than proof that the item has been assigned to your account. PikoBuy’s stated process is to wait for its warehouse to receive the goods, take photographs for confirmation and store the items. Allow for the platform’s own receipt record to appear, then compare it with the sender’s tracking details.

If a parcel is missing on arrival, PikoBuy’s terms direct the customer to contact the sender for verification and state that PikoBuy is not liable for missing items when the loss was not caused by PikoBuy. Preserve the seller’s dispatch evidence, carrier scans, carton count and any proof of delivery. Those records help identify whether the open question belongs with the sender, domestic carrier or warehouse.

Understand the inspection boundary

PikoBuy says all forwarded goods must be unpacked and inspected. Its forwarding terms also say that professional inspection is not available for special or professional products. Buyers are directed to review the inspection photos or purchase additional detailed photos. That makes photographs an important checkpoint, but not a complete technical test or authenticity certificate.

Compare visible facts: product type, colour, printed size, model label, quantity, included pieces, packaging condition and obvious damage. If a dimension or small label decides whether you can use the item, request a focused image rather than a vague “please check everything.” The PikoBuy QC photo guide explains what warehouse images can establish and what remains uncertain.

Keep seller after-sales responsibility with the seller

This is one of the most important forwarding limitations. PikoBuy’s shipping terms state that it does not provide after-sales service for forwarded products. The buyer must contact the sender or seller, while PikoBuy can only help ship goods back to that party. In practice, you should know the seller’s return conditions before directing an order to the warehouse.

If warehouse photos reveal a mismatch, document it without assumptions: show the ordered option, seller record and received photograph side by side. Contact the seller promptly and ask whether it will accept the return and which address to use. Do not remove tags, seals or packaging while the decision is open. The site’s general PikoBuy return policy guide covers purchases made through the agency workflow; forwarded orders have the separate after-sales boundary stated above.

Plan consolidation without assuming it is always cheaper

Sending several purchases to one warehouse can make organization easier, but consolidation is not proof of a lower total cost. International routes can use actual weight, dimensions or other billing methods, and product restrictions may limit which items can travel together. PikoBuy’s beginner guide says routes differ in delivery time and billing method, while its estimator asks for destination, product type, weight and parcel dimensions.

Wait for usable warehouse information before comparing parcel options. Identify fragile, bulky, battery-containing, liquid or otherwise restricted items accurately. Ask for only the packaging changes you actually need; PikoBuy’s guide lists minimal and reinforced packaging as examples of requests. Our PikoBuy shipping guide separates product cost, warehouse evidence and route selection so an estimate is not mistaken for a final charge.

Check legality, customs and destination charges

Warehouse acceptance does not mean an item is lawful to export, carry or import. PikoBuy’s Terms of Service prohibit illegal and infringing products, including goods without lawful intellectual-property rights or authorization, and describe a zero-tolerance policy toward counterfeit goods. The terms allow actions such as order cancellation, refusal of service, return or disposal when violations are found.

The same terms say cross-border transactions may incur duties, taxes, brokerage fees or other charges for which the buyer is responsible. Research destination rules before purchasing, not after the goods reach the warehouse. Accurate product descriptions and supporting invoices are especially important for customs. Do not ask a seller or forwarder to disguise the nature, value or brand of an item.

Submit the international parcel as a new decision

After the forwarded goods appear on the order page, PikoBuy’s published process says to submit the parcel as usual and pay the shipping fee. Review the destination address, selected contents, declared information, packaging request, available route and quoted charge at that point. The original seller payment did not include this separate international shipping step unless your own transaction explicitly says otherwise.

PikoBuy says its parcels are carried by third-party logistics providers and describes customs action, damage, loss and peak-season delay as cross-border risks outside its control. Its beginner guide says tracking information will be available within three days after dispatch. Neither statement guarantees a delivery date or outcome. Save the submitted parcel record and follow the actual tracking events for your shipment.

A forwarding checklist worth keeping

Before the domestic shipment leaves, confirm the current PikoBuy warehouse address, your identifying reference, the forwarding form, prepaid-versus-COD status and every domestic tracking number. Save the seller order, exact variants, quantities and return contact. After receipt, match the carton count and visible goods to that evidence, then resolve discrepancies before creating an international parcel.

Finally, keep three totals separate: the seller payment, domestic freight or COD charge, and international parcel charge. Add potential taxes and duties as destination costs rather than pretending they are known in advance. This ledger makes a forwarded purchase easier to audit and prevents a low item price from being mistaken for the delivered cost.

The practical view of PikoBuy forwarding

PikoBuy forwarding can bridge an independently purchased China order and an overseas address, but the buyer remains the organizer of the original seller relationship. The strongest workflow is built on precise address data, an incoming-parcel record, warehouse photo review and a separate international shipping decision. Each handoff should have evidence and a clearly responsible party.
