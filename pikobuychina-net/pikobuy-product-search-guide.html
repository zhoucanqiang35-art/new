PikoBuy Product Search Guide: Submit China Shopping Links Correctly

A source link is only the beginning. Preserve the exact seller, option and evidence so the item you research is the item you ask PikoBuy to purchase.

Independent research · reviewed 13 September 2026

PikoBuy product search is designed for people who find something on a Chinese marketplace but need help purchasing and shipping it internationally. According to PikoBuy’s current public pages, a buyer can search directly, submit a product link, use keywords, provide an image or send a sourcing request. The practical challenge is not merely making a product card appear. It is preserving enough detail to know that the option submitted matches the one you researched.

This guide focuses on that first decision stage. It does not promise that a seller has stock, that a product is lawful to import, that an image match is identical or that the displayed price will become the final delivered cost. PikoBuy’s beginner guide says prices are subject to the actual purchase and describes international shipping as a later payment. Treat every search result as a candidate that still needs live verification.

Choose the strongest search input you have

A direct product link is normally the most specific starting point because it points to a particular seller page. PikoBuy’s beginner guide tells users to paste a product link or keywords into its search box. Its homepage adds images and sourcing requests as other ways to discover an item. These routes serve different situations, and they do not carry the same amount of evidence.

Use a link when you know the exact listing. Use keywords when the source link is unavailable or when you want to compare possible sellers. Use an image or sourcing request when visual identification is the only useful clue. A visual match can lead to a similar product rather than the same material, dimensions, included parts or seller. The less specific the input, the more confirmation you need before ordering.

Prepare a Taobao, Tmall, 1688, Weidian or Yupoo link

PikoBuy’s guide specifically names Taobao, Tmall, 1688, Weidian and Yupoo among the platforms where a buyer may select products. Before copying a link, open the item page and note the store, full product title and date. Save a screenshot of the option area and any size or specification table that influences your decision. PikoBuy itself recommends saving item or store information for easier ordering.

Keep the original source URL even after PikoBuy creates or displays a product record. A shared or shortened link may later redirect differently, and a seller can revise a listing. Your saved record shows what you reviewed at a particular time; it does not freeze the offer. Reopen the live seller page immediately before paying and compare it with the PikoBuy order screen.

Record the exact option before you search

A product page may contain several colours, sizes, bundles or versions under one URL. Write down the exact combination you intend to buy: colour, size, model, quantity and included pieces. If a seller uses option codes rather than descriptive names, preserve the code and the matching image. Do not write only “the black one” when several photographs show black items with different specifications.

PikoBuy’s official order sequence tells the buyer to select specifications including colour, size and quantity before submitting the purchasing order and making the first payment. That selection is more important than the hero image. Our product-detail checklist explains how to separate the general listing from the specific option you may receive.

Use keywords as a discovery method, not proof of identity

A useful keyword query combines the product type with a measurable or selectable attribute: material, model number, size range, colour, capacity or package quantity. Avoid adding assumptions such as “authentic,” “best quality” or “trusted seller” unless reliable evidence supports them. Search words can narrow candidates, but they cannot certify a seller or item.

When several results appear, compare like with like. Check the seller, option, measurements, included parts and the date of the information. A lower headline price may belong to a smaller size, deposit, accessory or different version. Move only the strongest candidates into a short research record. The PikoBuy spreadsheet guide provides a practical structure for marking evidence as confirmed, unclear or missing.

Treat image search and sourcing requests carefully

An image is useful when you do not know the Chinese product name or original marketplace page. Save the full, uncropped image when possible, then describe the visible features that matter: shape, colour, pattern, connector, closure or approximate dimensions. Remove private information from screenshots before submitting them. If the image contains several products, identify which object you mean.

Image similarity is not product equivalence. Two visually similar items can differ in material, construction, measurements, accessories and seller terms. When PikoBuy or a seller returns a candidate, rebuild the evidence from the new source listing. Confirm the exact option and current price instead of assuming that the result inherits every detail from the reference image.

Check whether the request is lawful and permitted

PikoBuy’s Terms of Service state that users must not request, list, purchase, ship or receive illegal, infringing, fraudulent or improper products. The terms specifically prohibit goods without lawful intellectual-property rights or authorisation and describe a zero-tolerance approach to counterfeit and infringing products. PikoBuy may cancel orders, refuse service, return or dispose of items under the published terms.

A product appearing in search is not permission to buy or import it. Laws, carrier restrictions and customs rules can depend on the item and destination. Research those obligations before purchase, especially for batteries, liquids, food, medical items, branded goods or other regulated categories. This article does not determine whether a particular item is legal.

Compare the PikoBuy result with the source record

When PikoBuy displays the item, compare its title, seller, selected attributes, quantity and displayed price with your source evidence. If a translated title is vague, rely on the option code, measurement table and images rather than guessing. Resolve a mismatch before the purchasing order is submitted. A comment added after payment may not correct an option that was selected incorrectly.

PikoBuy’s Terms say that placing an order is an offer to purchase and that PikoBuy or participating merchants may accept or reject it. The beginner guide also notes that prices are subject to actual purchase and that an out-of-stock order will be refunded. These statements mean a searchable listing is not a stock reservation or final transaction. Record the amount actually presented at the purchase stage.

Understand what the first payment does not include

The official workflow separates the purchasing order from international shipping. First, the buyer selects the item specifications and completes the first payment. After warehouse receipt and inspection, the buyer chooses a suitable shipping route, submits the parcel and pays the international shipping fee. Taxes, duties, brokerage or other cross-border charges may also apply under PikoBuy’s Terms.

Do not compare candidates using item price alone. At the search stage, parcel weight, packaging dimensions and live route availability may still be unknown. Keep product-stage figures separate from parcel-stage figures. Our PikoBuy shipping guide explains how destination, product type, weight and dimensions enter the later decision.

Use warehouse inspection as the verification checkpoint

PikoBuy says warehouse processing includes check-in, photo confirmation and a defect check. When photographs become available, compare them with the exact option saved before purchase. Look for the visible label, colour, quantity, included pieces and obvious condition. Seller photographs explain the offer; warehouse photographs show the item received for your order. They answer different questions.

Photographs cannot establish everything. Fit, internal operation, composition and authenticity may require evidence beyond standard images. If a focused additional photo can answer a visible question, request the angle or measurement precisely. Use the method in our warehouse QC guide rather than treating a general inspection as a universal guarantee.

Act quickly when the evidence does not match

If the warehouse item appears different from the paid option, save the order record and relevant images and describe the discrepancy factually. PikoBuy’s return policy gives wrong colour or size and visible issues such as holes or stains as examples used to determine responsibility. Do not exaggerate what a photograph proves; show the paid selection beside the received evidence.

The current policy says eligible return requests may be submitted within five days after the order changes to “In Warehouse,” defined as 120 hours from the next hour. Seller consent, resale condition, category rules and possible fees also matter. Keep packaging and labels intact until the decision is settled, and consult our PikoBuy return guide for the full timing and evidence process.

A practical pre-payment checklist

Before submitting a purchasing order, confirm six items: the source domain and seller; the full product name; exact colour, size, model and quantity; the current option price; any measurement or included-part evidence; and whether the product appears permitted for purchase and import. Save the date and a screenshot. Then compare those fields with the PikoBuy order one final time.

If one non-negotiable detail remains unclear, pause and ask a specific question rather than hoping warehouse inspection will solve it. Search is a discovery and ordering bridge, not a quality promise. The best time to prevent the wrong purchase is before the first payment; the second-best time is at the warehouse checkpoint before international parcel submission.

The useful way to think about PikoBuy product search

PikoBuy product search can make Chinese marketplace links more workable for an international buyer by connecting discovery with purchasing assistance and a warehouse stage. Its value depends on the quality of the input and the discipline of the comparison. A precise link plus an exact option record creates a clearer order than an unexplained image or broad keyword.
