PikoBuy Payment Guide: Plan the Full Cost Before Ordering

The first checkout is not the delivered total. Separate the product order, warehouse decisions, international parcel payment and possible destination charges.

Independent research · reviewed 19 September 2026

A PikoBuy payment plan is easier to understand when you stop treating the transaction as one checkout. PikoBuy’s public beginner guide describes a first payment when the purchasing order is submitted and a later payment for international shipping after warehouse inspection. Its Terms of Service also warn that cross-border transactions may involve duties, taxes, brokerage fees or other charges for which the buyer is responsible.

That means a product card price cannot honestly represent the final cost at your door. The useful approach is a staged ledger: record what is known at each point, keep estimates separate from paid amounts and avoid committing the entire budget to the first purchase. This guide explains the published payment stages without inventing a universal fee, exchange rate or checkout method.

Stage one is the purchasing order

PikoBuy’s beginner guide tells buyers to choose the product specifications, including colour, size and quantity, submit the purchasing order and complete the first payment. That payment is tied to the selected goods. Before paying, compare the seller, exact option and quantity with your source record. A correct amount attached to the wrong variant is still the wrong purchase.

The same guide says prices are subject to the actual purchase and that an out-of-stock order will be refunded. PikoBuy’s Terms add that placing an order is an offer to purchase and that PikoBuy or a participating merchant may accept or reject it. A search result or displayed listing therefore should not be treated as a guaranteed stock reservation. Our product search guide shows how to preserve the evidence behind the order.

Know what the first payment does not settle

The first payment does not complete the international delivery workflow described by PikoBuy. Goods still need to reach the China warehouse, be checked in and photographed, and then be placed into a parcel for an available route. The international shipping fee is paid at that later stage. Destination charges may remain outside both of those payments.

Keep at least four columns in your budget: product purchase, domestic or warehouse-related costs, international parcel charge and destination-side costs. Do not combine an estimate with an amount already paid. Our PikoBuy spreadsheet guide provides a practical structure for recording source evidence and cost status as confirmed, estimated or still unknown.

Check the live payment options at checkout

PikoBuy’s current public pages display payment logos for Visa, Mastercard, American Express and JCB. A logo on a public page is not enough to prove that every card, country, currency or transaction will be accepted. The actual options shown in the buyer’s checkout, along with the issuer’s decision, are the relevant evidence for a real order.

Before confirming payment, read the currency, amount and payment description on the live screen. Check whether your bank or payment provider may apply its own conversion or international-transaction terms. This article does not claim a PikoBuy exchange rate or payment-provider fee because the public sources reviewed do not publish one universal figure. Save the successful receipt rather than relying on a pending banking notification.

Record the exact product amount

Save the order number, date, seller, selected variant, quantity, product amount and any domestic seller freight shown. If a discount changes the total, preserve the final checkout record. For several items, keep a line-by-line breakdown instead of one unexplained total. This makes a later refund, discrepancy or customs question easier to trace.

PikoBuy’s return policy says the total refund is based on the actual product price paid. That makes the payment record important. It also means you should not assume that every cost connected with the order will be refunded together with the product amount. Shipping, service and destination costs have separate rules and may involve different parties.

Use the warehouse stage as a financial decision point

PikoBuy says warehouse processing includes check-in, photo confirmation and a defect check. Review the images before paying to move the item internationally. Compare visible colour, size label, model, quantity, included pieces and condition with the paid order. International shipping should not become a reason to ignore a clear mismatch already visible in China.

If a focused photograph can answer an important visible question, request the necessary detail before parcel submission. Standard photos cannot prove fit, internal performance, material composition or authenticity in every case. The PikoBuy QC photo guide explains how to make a decision from evidence without treating warehouse images as a universal guarantee.

Understand possible return costs before you need a return

PikoBuy’s current policy says an unconditional return can include shipping back to the seller, the seller’s original shipping fee and a RMB 5 service fee. It notes that a buyer may still have to pay the first-delivery shipping cost even when the seller originally offered free shipping. Responsibility and seller agreement also affect the process.

For eligible agency orders, the policy gives a five-day warehouse application window, defined as 120 hours from the next hour after the status changes to “In Warehouse.” Returnability also depends on seller support, resale condition and product-category rules. If the order is wrong or unwanted, compare the likely return cost with the cost and risk of shipping it internationally. See the return policy guide for the full evidence checklist.

Forwarded goods follow a different payment trail

If you purchased directly from a seller and sent the parcel to PikoBuy, the original product payment happened outside PikoBuy’s purchasing-order workflow. Keep the seller invoice and payment proof with every domestic tracking number. PikoBuy’s forwarding terms say it does not provide after-sales service for forwarded products; the buyer must contact the seller or sender, while PikoBuy can help ship goods back.

The same forwarding terms state that cash-on-delivery domestic shipping fees will be deducted from the buyer’s PikoBuy account balance without confirmation and advise keeping sufficient balance. Confirm whether the sender is using prepaid or COD delivery before dispatch. Our PikoBuy forwarding guide separates the seller payment, domestic handoff and later international parcel payment.

Stage two is the international parcel payment

After inspection, PikoBuy’s guide tells the buyer to choose a suitable route, submit the parcel and pay the international shipping fee. The guide notes that routes differ in delivery time and billing methods. The shipping estimator asks for destination, product type, weight and three parcel dimensions, which shows why a product price alone cannot answer the later shipping question.

Use current warehouse information and live account options when comparing routes. Record the selected contents, packaging request, destination, quoted amount and payment receipt. Do not reuse an old estimate as though it were a current invoice. The PikoBuy shipping guide explains the parcel-planning stage in more detail.

Keep estimates and final charges visibly separate

An estimator is a planning tool. At the research stage, the final packed weight, dimensions, eligible route and account-specific checkout amount may not yet be known. Label every preliminary figure with the date, destination, product type and measurements used. When the live parcel checkout appears, replace the estimate in your ledger rather than storing both figures without explanation.

The same discipline applies to seller price changes. PikoBuy says prices are subject to actual purchase. If the confirmed amount differs from the listing you saved, pause and identify the reason before approving any change. Good budgeting is not predicting every charge; it is knowing which number came from which stage and whether it is estimated, requested, paid, refunded or still disputed.

Destination charges are a separate layer

PikoBuy’s Terms state that duties, taxes, brokerage fees and other charges may arise in cross-border transactions and are the buyer’s responsibility. The correct amount cannot be determined from a single global PikoBuy rule. It depends on current destination requirements and the actual goods. Research the receiving country or region before buying, using a truthful product description and value evidence.

Maintain a reserve rather than spending the entire budget on products and quoted freight. The PikoBuy customs guide explains how to preserve product identity, value and restricted-item evidence. Do not ask a seller, agent or forwarder to disguise the goods or invent a lower value. A misleading record creates risk rather than a reliable saving.

Payment success is not a delivery promise

PikoBuy’s shipping terms say parcels are carried by third-party logistics providers and describe customs action, damage, loss and peak-season delay as cross-border risks outside its control. Paying the parcel charge starts the selected logistics process; it does not guarantee a delivery date or outcome. Keep the parcel receipt, route record and tracking history.

The beginner guide says tracking information will be available within three days after dispatch. If the stated period passes without an update, document the parcel number and account status before contacting support. Avoid replacing the official record with assumptions from another buyer’s timeline, because a different destination, route, parcel or date is not evidence for your shipment.

A practical payment checklist

Before the first payment, confirm the seller, exact option, quantity, actual checkout amount and payment currency. Save the receipt. At the warehouse, compare the goods with the order and decide whether a return question needs action. Before the second payment, record the parcel contents, destination, packaging request, route, quoted international charge and any protection information shown.

Finally, add a separate destination-cost reserve and keep every later charge or refund connected to the correct order. If payment fails, PikoBuy’s Terms say the order will not be processed. Do not submit repeated payments blindly; first check the order status and payment record so a temporary failure is not confused with a completed transaction.

The practical view of PikoBuy payments

PikoBuy’s published workflow makes more sense as a sequence than as one price: purchase the exact goods, verify them at the warehouse, build an international parcel and prepare for possible destination charges. A simple staged ledger protects the budget from the most common misunderstanding—the belief that the first visible price is the complete delivered cost.
