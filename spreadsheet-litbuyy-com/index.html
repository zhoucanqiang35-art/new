QC, without overpromising

Six checks that make a photo review more useful.

  1. 01Confirm the selected colour, size and variant against your saved reference.
  2. 02Read the measurements rather than relying only on a label or standard size name.
  3. 03Inspect several angles for obvious stitching, shape, print or material issues.
  4. 04Ask about an unclear detail before parcel submission, while the order is still in the warehouse.
  5. 05Keep the photos and item record together in case you need to compare later.
  6. 06Treat photos as evidence of the visible item, not as a guarantee of authenticity or future performance.
