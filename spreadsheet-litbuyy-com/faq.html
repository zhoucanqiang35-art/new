FAQ · fact checked

Litbuy questions, answered with care.

These answers describe only what Litbuy’s public pages state or what follows directly from the published workflow. They are not guarantees for an individual order.

Practical answers

What the public Litbuy information supports.

Reviewed 21 September 2026. Live service terms, listings and shipping options can change, so check them before placing or submitting an order.

What does Litbuy say it does?

Litbuy publicly describes itself as a China shopping agent for Taobao, 1688 and Weidian. Its public pages also present QC checks, warehouse handling and international shipping as parts of its service.

Can products be sent to a Litbuy warehouse?

Yes. Litbuy’s public information describes items being sent to its warehouse, where they can be stored and prepared for shipping. The site also describes tracking storage, inspection and shipping progress for orders.

Can warehouse items be combined into one parcel?

Litbuy’s public information says items can be combined and shipped from the warehouse. Whether combining items is suitable depends on the items, destination and the current options shown when the parcel is ready.

Does Litbuy provide QC checks?

Litbuy publicly advertises QC checks. QC material can help compare visible details with the saved listing reference, but it should not be treated as a blanket guarantee of authenticity, material composition, fit, durability or future performance.

How long does Litbuy say free warehouse storage lasts?

Litbuy’s public service page states that each item receives 90 days of free storage from the day it arrives at the warehouse. Check the live service page for the current terms that apply to your order.

What is the forwarding flow?

Litbuy’s forwarding page outlines four practical stages: obtain the warehouse address, provide the relevant tracking information, submit the parcel and pay international shipping, then wait for delivery. The page says that a forwarding order ID is generated after the tracking number is submitted.

Can this site promise a shipping price or delivery time?

No. A final shipping price, route and transit time depend on the particular parcel, destination and live options at submission. This independent guide does not publish universal shipping promises.

Does Litbuy offer after-sales service?

Litbuy’s public pages present after-sales support as part of the service. The pages reviewed for this guide did not provide a universal refund or return period, so check the current order-specific terms before relying on a particular outcome.

Is this an official Litbuy website?

No. This is an independent product-discovery guide. It does not take payments, process purchases or make decisions for Litbuy. Its product links direct only to the FindSpreadsheet main database.

Where can I continue product research?

Use the category and product-detail routes on this site to continue in the FindSpreadsheet database. Save the original listing reference and selected variant before you compare any warehouse or QC information.
