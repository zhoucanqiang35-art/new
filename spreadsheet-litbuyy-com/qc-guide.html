QC photo guide

Review the visible details before you submit a parcel.

Warehouse photos are useful evidence of the item shown. They are not a guarantee of authenticity, quality or future performance.

  1. 01Confirm the selected colour, size and variant against the saved reference.
  2. 02Read the listed measurements instead of relying only on a label or size name.
  3. 03Check several angles for visible stitching, shape, print or material issues.
  4. 04Clarify an unclear detail while the item is still in the warehouse.
  5. 05Keep the product record and any photos together for later comparison.
